Galactica has implemented a zero-knowledge version of the [HTTP x402 payment standard on the Aztec testnet](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/4GTZTggnIPwLwgWwyF7gLg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtY3J5cHRvfGdhbGFjdGljYS1zaGlwcy1jb25maWRlbnRpYWwteDQwMi1wYXltZW50cy1vbi1henRlYy10ZXN0bmV0fHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL0dhbGFjdGljYU5ldC9zdGF0dXMvMjEwNjAxNDUyMDI4ODEzNzMwMD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20). The classic x402 standard uses the HTTP 402 "Payment Required" status code to trigger pay-per-request API calls for autonomous agents, but standard blockchain rails broadcast the consumer's wallet address, the provider's balance, and request frequency across public explorers.

Galactica’s implementation wraps the payment layer inside Aztec's zk-rollup proofs (deployed on Aztec 5.0.1 under Apache 2.0). The system obscures the sender, receiver, and transaction amounts while preserving atomic settlement and Ethereum mainnet finality. Regulatory requirements are shifted to an ingress layer: users complete identity checks once to generate a zero-knowledge KYC attestation, letting agents authenticate and clear micropayments to commercial API endpoints without leaking identity data or tracking transaction history on-chain.

Robinhood's offshore stock token product, which operates in more than 120 countries, would immediately exceed the proposed US caps if opened to domestic customers. Robinhood is building in-kind share redemption and automated shareholder voting into its equity token pipeline, alongside an active US rollout of perpetual futures covering eight crypto assets.

In Washington, President Trump appointed [former SEC Chair Jay Clayton to lead the Super Intelligence Force](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/aB9t7TPihpDBd6gTNL0Xlw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtY3J5cHRvfHJvYmluaG9vZC1xdWVzdGlvbnMtc2VjLWlubm92YXRpb24tY2Fwcy13aGlsZS1jbGF5dG9uLXRhcHBlZC1mb3ItYWktY3phcnx8aHR0cHM6Ly9kZWNyeXB0LmNvLzM4MDAxOS90cnVtcC1qYXktY2xheXRvbi1zZWMtcmlwcGxlLWFpLXB1c2gtc3VwZXItaW50ZWxsaWdlbmNlP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), an inter-agency task force tasked with organizing federal AI oversight. Clayton, who initiated 57 crypto enforcement actions during his prior SEC term - including the Ripple lawsuit - and whose SDNY office pursued the prosecution of Tornado Cash developer Roman Storm, will steer national security, software safety, and commercial controls across the federal AI apparatus.

Spot markets pushed Bitcoin past the $87,000 mark after traders exhausted and pulled an aggressive $85,000 ask wall on major order books. The move caps a 14.6 % rebound from mid-September lows, catalyzed by weak domestic labor figures that lowered the probability of an October Federal Reserve interest rate hike.

Institutional capital flows responded immediately. [US spot Bitcoin ETFs drew $134.4 million in net inflows](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/QtEUBJtnqvM7IyFrm-tXNw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtY3J5cHRvfGJpdGNvaW4tYnJlYWtzLTg3MDAwLWFzLW1hY3JvLWRhdGEtZHJpdmVzLWV0Zi1saXF1aWRpdHl8fGh0dHBzOi8vZGVjcnlwdC5jby8zODAwMDcvdXB0b2Jlci1zdGFydHMtZ3JlZW4tYml0Y29pbi1ldGZzLWRyYXctMTM0LW1pbGxpb24_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) over the first two days of the month ($102.7 million on Thursday, $31.7 million on Friday). The inflows reversed a $148.7 million single-day outflow logged on September 30, closing out a month that netted $2.65 billion in aggregate inflows across all approved spot issuers.

The European Central Bank published a strategy framework outlining [three models for central bank money on DLT](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/WVSJUG5mX0Jls3vIOUMF8A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtY3J5cHRvfGNlbnRyYWwtYmFuay1yYWlscy1hbmQtbWFya2V0LXN0cnVjdHVyZS1wb2xpY3l8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRoZWJsb2NrLmNvL25ld3MvcmVndWxhdGlvbi8yMDI2LTEwLTAyLWVjYi1vdXRsaW5lcy10aHJlZS1tb2RlbHMtZm9yLXB1dHRpbmctY2VudHJhbC1iYW5rLW1vbmV5LW9uY2hhaW4tNDE3NTUyP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ).
