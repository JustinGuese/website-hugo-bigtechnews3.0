Until now, only smaller models got cheaper. The new models also run about 30% faster, so the same chip turns out more tokens for the same hourly cost. The price cut roughly matches those savings, which Anthropic could have kept as margin. Expect more cuts like it as models keep getting cheaper to run.

That's the pricing signal. [SaaStr's argument](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/87LOuc-kJQ1b7_UF3PeI2A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctZm91bmRlcnN8YWdlbnRzLXdpbGwtcm91dGUtYXJvdW5kLXlvdXItcHJpY2luZy10aGUtYjJiLXdha2UtdXAtY2FsbHx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc2Fhc3RyLmNvbS9pZi15b3VyZS1yZWZvdW5kaW5nLXlvdXItYjJiLWNvbXBhbnktZm9yLWFpLW1ha2UteW91ci1wcm9kdWN0LWNoZWFwZXItcHJvYmFibHktbXVjaC1jaGVhcGVyLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) is brutal and simple: AI agents measure cost per call and will route around expensive products. The evidence is concrete. One vendor's bill rose 40% from AI usage - 35,000 calls per day - but the company cut calls by 90% due to cost, effectively pricing the vendor out of its own usage.

This breaks the pricing model most B2B software was built on. Human users stop paying attention after they sign; they don't check per-seat or per-call costs once the contract is executed. Agents check the cost of every call and call less when a product costs too much. For engineers building or operating AI-facing APIs, this means pricing models designed for human inattention fail with agents. The fix is to price agent usage apart from human seats and watch calls per customer per day as the leading indicator of churn risk. If that number drops, your product is being priced out of the agent economy.

[WorkOS launched Atlas](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/_jW90o00cpVrhogNaY4vTQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctZm91bmRlcnN8d29ya29zLWF0bGFzLWEtc2VjdXJlLWhvbWUtZm9yLWFpLWJ1aWx0LWludGVybmFsLWFwcHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd29ya29zLmNvbS9ibG9nL2F0bGFzLWFwcHM_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), a platform providing a secure home for internal AI-built apps, with early access opening this week. Atlas integrates with coding agents like Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex, allowing users to describe an app and have it deployed with company sign-in, data connections, and centrally managed access.

This solves the post-build problems of hosting, authentication, and API key management for internal tools. When every engineer can spin up an app with an agent, the security risk shifts from "how do we build this" to "how do we secure and govern all of these." Atlas gives admins a view of every running app and its owner, and they can limit sensitive apps to certain groups. For engineers, this is the missing piece between "my agent built a tool" and "my company can safely run it in production."

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/wGsJGu9HB3kS7AKeZ8p8Kg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctZm91bmRlcnN8dG9rZW4tZnJhdWQtaXMtZWF0aW5nLWFpLW1hcmdpbnMtY2hhdHByZHMtcGxheWJvb2t8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmNoYXRwcmQuYWkvaG93LWktYWkvdG9rZW4tZnJhdWQtaXMtZWF0aW5nLWFpLW1hcmdpbnMtaGVyZS1zLWhvdy1jbGFpcmUtdm8taXMtZmlnaHRpbmctaXQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: chatprd.ai ↗</a></p>

[Claire Vo's account](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/wGsJGu9HB3kS7AKeZ8p8Kg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctZm91bmRlcnN8dG9rZW4tZnJhdWQtaXMtZWF0aW5nLWFpLW1hcmdpbnMtY2hhdHByZHMtcGxheWJvb2t8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmNoYXRwcmQuYWkvaG93LWktYWkvdG9rZW4tZnJhdWQtaXMtZWF0aW5nLWFpLW1hcmdpbnMtaGVyZS1zLWhvdy1jbGFpcmUtdm8taXMtZmlnaHRpbmctaXQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) of token fraud costing tens of thousands of dollars is a warning for anyone running AI products. The attacks include trial farms, promo resale, proxy businesses, card abuse, persistent MCP workers, and model probing. Her defenses: turning off free trials, using Vercel AI Gateway, Stripe Radar, BotID, firewall rules, and MCP controls, plus AI fraud classification with hourly reviews.

For engineers building AI products, this is the playbook for protecting margins. The attack surface is wider than traditional SaaS because tokens have direct value - attackers can resell access or probe models for training data. The lesson: free trials are a fraud magnet, and you need multiple layers of defense including AI-based fraud classification that reviews usage patterns hourly, not weekly.
