A new paper from Google researchers on [arXiv](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/-ou0azXrZQH8Eao0ErZeIA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjctYWl8Z29vZ2xlLWRldGFpbHMtZGF0YS1mcmVlLW9uLXBvbGljeS1zZWxmLXRyYWluaW5nfHxodHRwczovL2FyeGl2Lm9yZy9hYnMvMjYxMC4zMjc3MD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) details "On-Policy Self-Training," an approach to foundation model post-training that eliminates human-curated datasets. Guided only by broad, high-level topical seeds, the model autonomously writes synthetic question-and-answer pairs, generates privileged reasoning traces, and evaluates the resulting paths against internal consistency metrics.
