> The interesting question in agent commerce is not which model closes the sale but who owns the identity and payment rails when the customer is software.

<h2 id="agents-become-customers">Agents become customers</h2>

Two stories point the same direction. **Anthropic's Skills over MCP public beta** lets MCP servers ship multi-step workflow skills, with Tasks and Elicitation now GA - the agent platform is becoming a distribution surface, not just a chat window. And **Stripe's integration with Claude Duet** formalizes the idea that software will transact as a customer. The non-obvious beneficiary is not the model vendor; it is whoever owns authentication and payment rails, because agent-driven transactions break the assumptions (cookies, card-on-file, human-in-the-loop 3DS) that current flows are built on. That is a payments-infrastructure problem, and it is why we watch Shopify's exposure here even though today's item names Stripe.

Separately, the **4,912-answer study on AI Overviews** showing Google citing YouTube in 44-70% of answers and Reddit in 35-63% - while ChatGPT cites neither - reinforces that Alphabet's search surface increasingly routes to properties it controls. That is a moat, not a leak, and it is consistent with our positive stance on the search business.
