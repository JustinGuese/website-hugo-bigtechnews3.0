[Ramp launched Accounts Receivable](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/UWRwVMMfOapHN6LBHlGJsg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjQtZmludGVjaHxyYW1wcy1hci1wbGF5LXRhcmdldHMtdGhlLTI3LWRheS1jYXNoLWJ1ZmZlcnx8aHR0cHM6Ly9yYW1wLmNvbS9ibG9nL2ludHJvZHVjaW5nLWFjY291bnRzLXJlY2VpdmFibGU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), expanding from spend management into collections. The product extracts contract and purchase-order terms into invoice drafts, prepares follow-ups using customer context, matches incoming payments to open invoices, recognizes revenue, and syncs accounting records back to the ERP.

The pitch is that the median small business has just 27 days of cash buffer, and slow collections are a self-inflicted wound. The manual rekeying between billing, payments, and ERP systems is where the delay lives. This is a direct attack on the integration seams that plague finance teams, and it signals Ramp's ambition to be the single pane of glass for both money out and money in.

The [CLARITY Act's cloture vote failed 49 to 50](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zBcvfZPi64Asi_cFM1tiSg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjQtZmludGVjaHxjbGFyaXR5LWFjdC1kaWVzLWluLXRoZS1zZW5hdGUtNDktNTB8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmZpbnRlY2h3ZWVrbHkuY29tL25ld3MvY2xhcml0eS1hY3QtY2xvdHVyZS12b3RlLWZhaWxzLTQ5LTUwLXNlcHRlbWJlci0yMDI2P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) on September 15, short of the 60 needed. The path forward now shifts to agency action: exemptions, guidance, and rulemaking. A lame-duck session post-midterms is the most plausible window, with passage odds in the single digits. The consequence for builders is regulatory uncertainty persists, and the EU's MiCA framework remains the clearer rulebook for shipping regulated products.

WislaCod's CEO makes the case that [core banking programmes don't go over budget because of the ledger](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/SnMyLO9wiqos6QYHl5dflg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjQtZmludGVjaHxjb3JlLWJhbmtpbmctb3ZlcnJ1bnMtaXRzLXRoZS1zZWFtcy1ub3QtdGhlLWxlZGdlcnx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZmluZXh0cmEuY29tL2Jsb2dwb3N0aW5nLzMyOTYxL3doZXJlLWEtY29yZS1iYW5raW5nLW92ZXJydW4tYWN0dWFsbHktZ29lcy13aGF0LXRoZS1wcmltYXJ5LXJlY29yZC1zYXlzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). The cost lives in the "seams": platform decisions, migration timing, integration, testing, and unmanaged vendor chains.

The evidence is specific. RTGS came in £56M over budget. TSB's failed migration came down to a login misconfiguration and poor visibility into 85 subcontractors, not the data migration itself. Any engineer who has lived through a core swap knows this is right: the ledger is the easy part, the integration hell around it is where budgets die.

India's UPI looked like a textbook Christensen disruptor: cheap, basic, aimed at the low end. Yet [India's credit card business has tripled in five years](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3aPYUBgbJoiAGqZvDCc5tw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjQtZmludGVjaHx1cGktaGFzbnQta2lsbGVkLWNyZWRpdC1jYXJkcy1hbmQtaGVyZXMtd2h5fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5maW5leHRyYS5jb20vYmxvZ3Bvc3RpbmcvMzI5NTUvZml2ZS1yZWFzb25zLXdoeS11cGktaGFzLW5vdC1kaXNydXB0ZWQtY3JlZGl0LWNhcmQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t).

The reasons are structural. Banks earn nothing on UPI but 2-3% on cards, so they push cards hard. UPI scams drove some users back to cards for fraud protection. And UPI was never designed to compete: no rewards, no deferred payment, no fraud protection, treated as a public good. That may start to shift on October 15, when merchant UPI payments above INR 2,000 begin carrying a 0.4% fee.

[Numeric launched its Financial Data Platform](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/OWhlYuPzL9o9xw3_mPaWTg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjQtZmludGVjaHxudW1lcmljLXJldGhpbmtzLXRoZS1sZWRnZXItZm9yLXRoZS1hZ2VudC1lcmF8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3Lm1vcm5pbmdzdGFyLmNvbS9uZXdzL2FjY2Vzc3dpcmUvMTIyNDk1N21zbi9udW1lcmljLWxhdW5jaGVzLWZpbmFuY2lhbC1kYXRhLXBsYXRmb3JtLXRoZS1lcnAtcmVwbGFjZW1lbnQtYnVpbHQtZm9yLXRoZS1hZ2VudC1lcmE_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), an ERP replacement that stores each business event as a complete record with source documents and metadata, rather than compressing it into debits and credits. This architecture is closer to a data warehouse with accounting logic than a traditional ledger.

The payoff is a close measured in hours and queryable detail without exporting to a separate FP&A stack. CEO Parker Gilbert argues AI-native ERPs are just "same house, new paint." For engineers, this removes the context ceiling that limits automation in traditional ledgers, and it allows module-by-module migration from systems like NetSuite.
