The company launched [Astra](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Yz4VQlHL0LRwZoRqll5Nxw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDctaXR8fHxodHRwczovL3ZlbnR1cmViZWF0LmNvbS90ZWNobm9sb2d5L3dlbGNvbWUtdG8tdGhlLWFnaS1lcmEtb3BlbmFpLWxhdW5jaGVzLWdwdC02LWFzdHJhP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), a model built for longer-running work that operates software across browsers, spreadsheets, websites, and desktop applications. This is not a chatbot that answers questions - it is a system that clicks, types, and navigates on your behalf. For engineers, this shifts the design question from "how do I prompt a model" to "how do I control one that can do anything I can do in a GUI." Authorization, audit logging, and rollback take on new weight when the actor is autonomous.

Snowflake's earnings call delivered the clearest signal yet that enterprise AI spending is bifurcating. The company's [CoCo coding assistant](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/KvADb3ylhlsyX-Bud-4bjw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDctaXR8fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5pdGlnZXIuY29tL25ld3MvMTE1MTM4MzE3NT91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) (formerly Cortex Code) hit 9,100 enterprise customer accounts - up at least 28% quarter-over-quarter - and now runs on Anthropic and OpenAI models while integrating open-weight options like DeepSeek-V4-Flash and GLM-5.3. The driver is not enthusiasm for Snowflake specifically; it is CIOs fleeing Anthropic after price increases and refusing to bet on a single frontier model provider. Snowflake positions itself as the data-layer intermediary that makes multi-model access operationally coherent. If you are evaluating coding assistants for an enterprise team, the question is no longer "which model" but "which platform gives you switching leverage."

Okta, Auth0, and Descope all shipped [Cross App Access implementations](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/jQ7uA3g3lZA33Z4ow87nDQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDctaXR8dGhyZWUtaWRlbnRpdHktdmVuZG9ycy1jb252ZXJnZWQtb24tdGhlLXNhbWUtcGF0dGVybi1pbi1laWdodC1kYXlzfHxodHRwczovL3dvcmtvcy5jb20vYmxvZy9jcm9zcy1hcHAtYWNjZXNzLWNvbnZlcmdlZC1pbi1laWdodC1kYXlzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) between August 24 and September 1. That is not coincidence - it is an industry recognizing a problem that agentic AI has made urgent: how do you let an AI agent reach a customer's apps without sharing the customer's identity credentials? The answer they landed on is a two-layer pattern built on two open standards. The first is a Client ID Metadata Document that tells the target app who is calling. The second is an ID-JAG token exchange where the customer's IdP authorizes which apps the agent may reach. Okta's Agent SSO is already GA in core Okta plans with integrations for Anthropic's Claude, Slack, Notion, Datadog, and others. Auth0 promoted its requesting-side ID-JAG out of beta. Descope added self-service XAA alongside SSO and SCIM. For B2B teams building or buying agent products, the takeaway is blunt: whether your authorization server speaks this two-layer pattern matters more than which identity vendor you picked.
