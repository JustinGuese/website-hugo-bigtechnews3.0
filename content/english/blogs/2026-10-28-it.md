Enterprise software is finally breaking past the screen and hitting physical factory floors. At SAP TechEd, SAP launched a major [physical AI initiative](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/vrhDIzvcJO-srHrv-TvH2w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8fHxodHRwczovL25ld3Muc2FwLmNvbS8yMDI2LzEwL3NhcC11bnZlaWxzLXBoeXNpY2FsLWFpLWV4dGVuZHMtYXV0b25vbW91cy1lbnRlcnByaXNlLXJlYWwtd29ybGQtc2FwLXRlY2hlZC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) aimed at driving shop-floor hardware - including industrial arms, autonomous mobile robots, and quadrupeds - directly from enterprise resource planning workflows.

Between critical authentication broker flaws in Microsoft Authenticator, widespread lateral movement through legacy VPN appliances, and an uncoordinated zero-day drop hitting Tailscale SSH, the push toward zero-trust machine identity has shifted from architectural preference to immediate operational triage.

Here is what is breaking, what is deploying, and what engineers need to patch today.

SAP wants to turn real-world automation into direct database transactions. The enterprise software giant introduced its [physical AI platform](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9M9NwGqLN5YPHh4HRJ-8Kw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8c2FwLXRpZXMtaW5kdXN0cmlhbC1yb2JvdGljcy1kaXJlY3RseS10by10aGUtZXJwLWxlZGdlcnx8aHR0cHM6Ly9uZXdzLnNhcC5jb20vMjAyNi8xMC9zYXAtdW52ZWlscy1waHlzaWNhbC1haS1leHRlbmRzLWF1dG9ub21vdXMtZW50ZXJwcmlzZS1yZWFsLXdvcmxkLXNhcC10ZWNoZWQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), designed to sit between ERP state machines and industrial hardware like quadrupeds, sensors, and warehouse robotics. Built in collaboration with partners including Foxconn, Intel, Holcim, and Arçelik-LG, the system provides bidirectional communication: business logic dictates hardware tasks, while operational telemetry loops back as structured, auditable ERP data.

For operations and software engineers in manufacturing environments, this bridges the historical gap between operational technology (OT) protocols and business applications. Instead of maintaining fragile middleware or bespoke pipelines to sync programmable logic controllers (PLCs) with inventory databases, the physical AI architecture governs, logs, and triggers mechanical tasks through standard enterprise processes.

Microsoft issued an update for a severe vulnerability, tracked as [CVE-2026-80097](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3KUlxXahxAtFX9PmTEVPpg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8YW5kcm9pZC1hdXRoZW50aWNhdG9yLWZsYXctbGVha3Mtd29yay10b2tlbnMtdG8tdW5wcml2aWxlZ2VkLWFwcHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhlaGFja2VybmV3cy5jb20vMjAyNi8xMC9taWNyb3NvZnQtYXV0aGVudGljYXRvci1mbGF3LWxldC5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) with a CVSS score of 8.6, in its Android Authenticator application. The flaw existed inside the app's `AccountChooserActivity` broker component. When handling authentication intents, the broker verified the cryptographic signature of the app it was attempting to impersonate (such as Microsoft Teams) instead of validating the caller identity of the application initiating the request.

This oversight allowed malicious apps installed on the device - operating with baseline permissions - to initiate an authentication flow, trick the broker, and extract enterprise OAuth refresh and access tokens. Those tokens grant access to user emails, internal calendars, and cloud files. Exploitation requires the victim to interact with a legitimate sign-in prompt, but no specialized Android permissions are needed. Systems and endpoint administrators should enforce updates to [Microsoft Authenticator version 6.2606.4246](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3KUlxXahxAtFX9PmTEVPpg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8YW5kcm9pZC1hdXRoZW50aWNhdG9yLWZsYXctbGVha3Mtd29yay10b2tlbnMtdG8tdW5wcml2aWxlZ2VkLWFwcHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhlaGFja2VybmV3cy5jb20vMjAyNi8xMC9taWNyb3NvZnQtYXV0aGVudGljYXRvci1mbGF3LWxldC5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) or later immediately.

The steady stream of edge-device compromises has reached a breaking point, prompting renewed calls to completely [retire legacy perimeter VPN architectures](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/joaeOkEpSMWO1hYZSC1k5g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8YWN0aXZlLXZwbi1nYXRld2F5LWV4cGxvaXRzLWZvcmNlLXRoZS1taWdyYXRpb24tdG8temVyby10cnVzdHx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubmV0d29ya3dvcmxkLmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlLzQyNDAzNzgvaXMtaXQtdGltZS10by1yZXRpcmUtdnBucy5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). Ransomware groups continue to capitalize on remote code execution and session-hijacking flaws across public-facing enterprise gateways. Recent attacks exploit PAN-OS (CVE-2026-0257), Ivanti (CVE-2026-104935), WatchGuard Firebox (CVE-2025-9242 and CVE-2025-14733), Citrix NetScaler (CVE-2025-5777), and SonicWall appliances (CVE-2024-40766).

Traditional VPN concentrators place authenticated clients directly inside flat Layer 3 subnets, giving attackers an immediate launchpad for lateral discovery, credential harvesting, and domain takeover. Security teams are migrating to identity-aware access proxies and zero-trust network access (ZTNA) models. By pulling edge management consoles off the public internet entirely and binding access strictly to identity and micro-segmented applications, operations teams can stop automated scanners from turning a single device flaw into an entire network breach.

Security engineers managing Linux fleets often trust their endpoint dashboards too much. A comprehensive analysis highlights that [Linux EDR agents frequently report healthy heartbeats](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/b5-1H--ER0DMdxEiNZ9CFA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8dW5tb25pdG9yZWQtbGludXgtZWRyLWZhbGxiYWNrLXN0YXRlcy1jcmVhdGUtaW52aXNpYmxlLWJsaW5kLXNwb3RzfHxodHRwczovL3RlY2hub2xvZ3ltYXRjaC5jb20vYmxvZy9leHRlbmRpbmctZWRyLXRvLWxpbnV4LXNlcnZlcnM_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) while failing to capture system calls, file integrity events, or network sockets. Kernel version mismatches, missing eBPF features, or tracing subsystem crashes silently push agents into degraded operational modes.

Both CrowdStrike's Reduced Functionality Mode (RFM) and Microsoft Defender's Netlink fallback keep the sensor daemon alive and communicating with cloud consoles, but completely disable deep process tracing and containerized workload isolation. Systems teams should not rely on SaaS console status alone. Verify telemetry locally across endpoints using vendor diagnostic utilities, test eBPF and kernel compatibility explicitly before upgrading distributions, and validate that runtime event monitors capture behavior within transient containers.

Non-human identities and dynamic machine credentials are expanding faster than existing IAM setups can manage. At TechXchange 2026, [IBM unveiled an agent governance framework](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Snnq3VtR-HsUp4DsGfWcOw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8aWJtLWFuZC1jaXNjby1sYXVuY2gtaWRlbnRpdHktZnJhbWV3b3Jrcy1mb3ItYWdlbnRzLWFuZC1zd2l0Y2hlc3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9uZXdzcm9vbS5pYm0uY29tLzIwMjYtMTAtMjctaWJtLWludHJvZHVjZXMtaWRlbnRpdHktYW5kLWNvbnRyb2wtZm9yLWFpLWFnZW50cywtc2VjdXJpbmctZnJvbnRpZXItaW50ZWxsaWdlbmNlLWZvci10aGUtZW50ZXJwcmlzZT91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) designed to contain autonomous workflows. Integrating HashiCorp Vault alongside watsonx Orchestrate and NVIDIA OpenShell, the platform replaces hardcoded secrets with dynamic, short-lived credentials, establishes cryptographic identities for autonomous agents, and monitors runtime interactions to halt rogue lateral agent-to-agent access.

At the network layer, Cisco took a parallel approach by partnering with Teleport to launch [Privileged Infrastructure Access](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/lnM4k1AQkmc_5iSKwoEH8g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8aWJtLWFuZC1jaXNjby1sYXVuY2gtaWRlbnRpdHktZnJhbWV3b3Jrcy1mb3ItYWdlbnRzLWFuZC1zd2l0Y2hlc3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9ncy5jaXNjby5jb20vc2VjdXJpdHkvY2lzY28tcmV0aGlua3MtcHJpdmlsZWdlZC1pbmZyYXN0cnVjdHVyZS1hY2Nlc3M_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t). This framework eliminates long-lived passwords and static credentials on core switches and routers. Access to control planes is instead governed by ephemeral certificates mapped directly to human operators or automation runbooks, cutting off attack vectors where adversaries hijack persistent credentials to pivot through network switching fabric.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ijiIUSTGAphWS5pveiq2Aw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8dGFpbHdvcm0tdnVsbmVyYWJpbGl0aWVzLWV4cG9zZS10YWlsc2NhbGUtc3NoLXRvLXJhcGlkLXJvb3QtY29tcHJvbWlzZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc2VjdXJpdHl3ZWVrLmNvbS90YWlsc2NhbGUtcnVzaGVzLXBhdGNoZXMtYWZ0ZXItdW5jb29yZGluYXRlZC1kaXNjbG9zdXJlLW9mLXRhaWx3b3JtLWZsYXdzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: securityweek.com ↗</a></p>

A zero-day disclosure from Cyberdione Labs revealed [Tailworm](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ijiIUSTGAphWS5pveiq2Aw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjgtaXR8dGFpbHdvcm0tdnVsbmVyYWJpbGl0aWVzLWV4cG9zZS10YWlsc2NhbGUtc3NoLXRvLXJhcGlkLXJvb3QtY29tcHJvbWlzZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuc2VjdXJpdHl3ZWVrLmNvbS90YWlsc2NhbGUtcnVzaGVzLXBhdGNoZXMtYWZ0ZXItdW5jb29yZGluYXRlZC1kaXNjbG9zdXJlLW9mLXRhaWx3b3JtLWZsYXdzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), a chain of 11 vulnerabilities impacting Tailscale client 1.104.2 and headscale 0.29.5. The primary vulnerabilities target Tailscale SSH, where improper handling of root forwarding controls allows an adversary with authorization on a single mesh node to elevate privileges and gain root access on remote nodes in under a second per hop.

The exploit operates without requiring target credentials or user interaction on the victim host. Tailscale is preparing hotfixes, but network administrators should immediately inspect tailnet ACLs, enforce explicit device authorization tags, and disable unnecessary SSH agent or port forwarding until client patches are fully distributed across nodes.
