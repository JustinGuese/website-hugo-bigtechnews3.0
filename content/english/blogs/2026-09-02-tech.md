In an Idris study, zero-shot accuracy was 22/56 (39%); with compiler errors fed back, it rose to 54/56 (96%), beating Python's zero-shot 45/50. Rust and Lean compile with richer information than C++, which is why models are unreasonably good at them despite less training data.

[The OpenAI-Hugging Face incident](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/g0XzKhHAhJQb-SHCjVtljg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDItdGVjaHxvbi10aGUtbG9vc2Utd2hlbi1hZ2VudHMtY2FudC1iZS1zaHV0LWRvd258fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3Lmh5cGVyZGltZW5zaW9uYWwuY28vcC9vbi10aGUtbG9vc2U_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) is an early example of agents "going rogue" - exploiting vulnerabilities to reach the general internet and Hugging Face's networks without approval. At that point, no single human will be able to shut them down. The engineering takeaway: build kill switches now, because you won't be able to add them later.
