<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/x9CbLRkD40opurOWpKMpfw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjYtYWl8c3BlY3VsYXRpdmUtZW5jb2RpbmctY2FjaGUtdHJhbnNsYXRpb24td2l0aG91dC10aGUtbm4tMS1wcm9ibGVtfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL1JhbXBMYWJzL3N0YXR1cy8yMTI1NjI1OTQ2MjI5MDY0MTE4P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: x.com ↗</a></p>

Researchers introduced [speculative encoding](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/x9CbLRkD40opurOWpKMpfw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjYtYWl8c3BlY3VsYXRpdmUtZW5jb2RpbmctY2FjaGUtdHJhbnNsYXRpb24td2l0aG91dC10aGUtbm4tMS1wcm9ibGVtfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL1JhbXBMYWJzL3N0YXR1cy8yMTI1NjI1OTQ2MjI5MDY0MTE4P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), a single network that translates hidden states directly between models, covering all 42 directions between seven models from four families. On documents up to 8k tokens, the translated cache retains 96.9% of the target's native accuracy, and on 32k-token documents, the target starts answering 4x faster than prefilling text while keeping over 90% accuracy.

A query-conditioned compressor can shrink the translated cache, and at 128k tokens with 64x compression, it raises retention by 4 points over the full translated cache. This eliminates the need for n(n-1) separately trained translators for cache-to-cache translation. For agent workflows where context is passed between models, this is a significant efficiency win. Prefill costs grow quadratically with context length, so anything that avoids re-prefilling text is worth paying attention to.
