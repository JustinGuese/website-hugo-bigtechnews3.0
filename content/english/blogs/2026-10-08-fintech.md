By packaging this engine for third-party loan servicers, Valon converted its internal servicing stack into pure enterprise infrastructure.

The CFTC unveiled an advance notice of proposed rulemaking introducing [Regulation CTX and Regulation CAM](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/dEw2J_yp0CBstdOOHhwrGQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDgtZmludGVjaHx0aGUtY2Z0Yy10YXJnZXRzLW1hcmdpbi1hbmQtY3VzdG9keS1hcmNoaXRlY3R1cmVzfHxodHRwczovL2RlY3J5cHQuY28vMzgwMDkwL2NmdGMtcGxhbi1jcnlwdG8tZXhjaGFuZ2VzLWZlZGVyYWwtb3ZlcnNpZ2h0LWxldmVyYWdlP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), designed to bring cryptocurrency exchanges under federal authority. With the statutory Clarity Act stuck in Congress, the agency is relying on a 2010 Dodd-Frank provision: any retail commodity transaction involving margin, financing, or leverage must happen on a registered CFTC exchange.

Crucially, the rule looks past marketing labels to assess asset delivery mechanics. If a retail trader buys an asset and the exchange holds the keys internally without immediate delivery to private user-controlled custody within 28 days, regulators can deem that transaction financed. Exchanges that skip leverage entirely can remain under state money-transmitter licensing, but anyone offering margin, synthetic exposure, or pooled omnibus custody will need to design systems for verifiable proof-of-reserves, formal broker-dealer AML controls, and compliant token listings.

Modern Treasury has [applied to the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC)](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/uE_JH6HOYIyCltHCeAIctQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDgtZmludGVjaHxtb2Rlcm4tdHJlYXN1cnktZmlsZXMtZm9yLWFuLW9jYy10cnVzdC1iYW5rLWNoYXJ0ZXJ8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3Lm1vZGVybnRyZWFzdXJ5LmNvbS9uZXdzcm9vbS9wcmVzcy1yZWxlYXNlcy9tb2Rlcm4tdHJlYXN1cnktYXBwbGllcy10by1lc3RhYmxpc2gtbmF0aW9uYWwtdHJ1c3QtYmFuaz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) to form Modern Treasury National Trust Bank. For system architects, this bridges a wide infrastructure gap. Right now, integrating fiat rails (ACH, FedNow, RTP) and stablecoins requires running distinct ledgers, disjointed regulatory compliance frameworks, and separate custody partners. Modern Treasury's charter will allow users to orchestrate bank payments and stablecoin balances through [a single, unified API](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zO1qrH42DV68ip6CCkyk2g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDgtZmludGVjaHxtb2Rlcm4tdHJlYXN1cnktZmlsZXMtZm9yLWFuLW9jYy10cnVzdC1iYW5rLWNoYXJ0ZXJ8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwNzE1NzQ3ODgxMDk5NzAyNy5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), moving compliance guarantees into the clearing layer.
