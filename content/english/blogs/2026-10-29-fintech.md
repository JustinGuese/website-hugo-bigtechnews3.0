Under the agreement, Kalshi gains access to benchmark cash index settlements, while S&P will ingest Kalshi’s order-book probabilities to create commercial data feeds covering macroeconomic metrics, political outcomes, and sports. 

The deal transforms event markets from retail speculation into institutional settlement infrastructure. Prediction platforms have spent years battling state gaming regulators and the CFTC over product legality. Tying contract expiries directly to Wall Street's primary benchmark administrator gives the asset class a deterministic pricing anchor and integrates event contracts straight into institutional data terminals.

The engineering lift shifts prediction markets onto traditional market data architecture. Instead of bespoke oracle resolution or manual committee sign-offs, Kalshi contracts will settle against published index ticks via deterministic data pipelines. For trading desks, it bridges raw event risk into risk management systems that already ingest standard S&P Dow Jones feeds.
