[Walraswap](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/fiy9AaY3qJh1_El6vZ-vAQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTAtY3J5cHRvfHdhbHJhc3dhcC10aGUtZGV4LXRoYXQtc2V0dGxlcy1ldmVyeXRoaW5nLWF0LW9uZS1wcmljZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly9ldGhyZXNlYXIuY2gvdC93YWxyYXN3YXAtZXF1aWxpYnJpdW0tYmFzZWQtZGVjZW50cmFsaXplZC1leGNoYW5nZS1pbnRlZ3JhdGluZy1leHRlcm5hbC1hbW0tbGlxdWlkaXR5LzI2MjE5P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is a proposed equilibrium-based batch DEX that settles all trade orders in a batch at a uniform clearing price. The key property: a price vector exists such that all submitted orders execute with no token deficit or surplus. That's a stronger guarantee than CoW Swap, UniswapX, or 1inch Fusion, which use uniform clearing prices alone.

Why this matters: CoW Swap's solvers can censor orders to shift clearing outcomes in their favor. Walraswap's equilibrium property requires including all available trade orders, closing that manipulation vector. The remaining attack surface is solver censorship itself, which the protocol treats as detectable in a repeated game - analogous to how block builders don't unbundle searcher bundles. The project started at Flashbots in 2023 and frames settlement as a convex optimization problem. Net trades at equilibrium are unique even when price vectors aren't.

For DEX operators, this is the next step past batch auctions. If it ships, it changes the game theory for solvers: you can't cherry-pick orders anymore, because the equilibrium price depends on the full order set.

[Base's Denim upgrade](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/c5E4YF89K1eR8INUytOKxQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTAtY3J5cHRvfGJhc2UtZGVuaW0tMjAwbXMtYmxvY2tzLWtpbGwtdGhlLXN0YWxlLXF1b3RlLXdpbmRvd3x8aHR0cHM6Ly94LmNvbS9iaWNvbm9teS9zdGF0dXMvMjExODcxNjI5NTQxOTkzMzAyMz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), scheduled for November 2026, cuts block time from 2 seconds to 200 milliseconds and retires flashblocks. That directly reduces the window where a proactive market maker's (propAMM) quoted price can go stale.

The execution risk for propAMMs is the gap between an onchain quoted price and the live market rate. On Ethereum's 12-second blocks, builders like Titan address this by guaranteeing a maker's latest price update lands before any competing trade in the same block - a mechanism Flashbots is standardizing as ERC-8324. Fermi has used this to process hundreds of millions in weekly DEX volume. On Base today, flashblocks preview transactions but don't finalize them, leaving a 2-second staleness window. Denim converts each 200ms slot into a full block, shrinking that window to 200ms.

For propAMM operators, the change is structural. Per-block exposure limits get more precise, and the execution advantage shifts from tip size to sequencer latency. Biconomy has been testing this with a major market maker on its own Base propAMM since October. On Ethereum, the problem persists: a large share of DEX volume trades at prices 12 seconds stale, and propAMMs still rely on builders to include price updates.

[The AI compute boom has a debt problem](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zUTyYmVESigElG7vOb5u_A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTAtY3J5cHRvfG9uY2hhaW4tY3JlZGl0LWlzLWZpbGxpbmctdGhlLWFpLWNvbXB1dGUtZmluYW5jaW5nLWdhcHx8aHR0cHM6Ly94LmNvbS9QaW5rQnJhaW5zX2lvL3N0YXR1cy8yMTE5NjczOTExMDUwOTAzNTY3P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). Banks lend to large neoclouds, leaving smaller operators with a few hundred GPUs unable to get debt. Data shows 110 public GPU/data center financings totaling $197B since 2023, with loan pricing from SOFR + 2.25% for investment-grade to SOFR + 4.5-5.5% for sub-investment-grade.

Onchain credit funded by stablecoin deposits is starting to fill the gap, using GPUs as collateral. USD.AI lends to small neoclouds at 10-15% rates (12% median) versus 9-10% fixed at banks, and says loans can clear in under 7 days. It has originated $531M across 20 financings.
