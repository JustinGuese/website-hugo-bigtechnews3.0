The right harness can cut the cost of the same AI result by 71% without losing accuracy. That's not a model upgrade or a cheaper API deal - it's the plumbing around the model, and it's now the biggest lever on your unit economics. A Berkeley study frames it as the next-generation software opportunity, and a hypothetical company using a harness spends $37k on inference versus $131k for a naive one, yielding a 75% gross margin versus 38%.

That changes what you build and what you sell. If the harness is the margin, then the moat isn't the model - it's your ability to compress common workflows into deterministic code and reserve expensive models for only the steps that need them. Engineers should care because the design of the harness, not just the model, is now the primary lever on unit economics for AI products.

<h2 id="the-most-important-product-decision-is-what-you-dont-build"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/sAw181RsUVdZPwmQGe9h9g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LXByb2R1Y3QtZGVjaXNpb24taXMtd2hhdC15b3UtZG9udC1idWlsZHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9saWFtbnVnZW50Lm1lL3Bvc3RzL3doYXQteW91LWRvbnQtYnVpbGQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Most Important Product Decision Is What You Don&#x27;t Build</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/sAw181RsUVdZPwmQGe9h9g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LXByb2R1Y3QtZGVjaXNpb24taXMtd2hhdC15b3UtZG9udC1idWlsZHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9saWFtbnVnZW50Lm1lL3Bvc3RzL3doYXQteW91LWRvbnQtYnVpbGQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: liamnugent.me ↗</a></p>

The [author's argument](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/sAw181RsUVdZPwmQGe9h9g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LXByb2R1Y3QtZGVjaXNpb24taXMtd2hhdC15b3UtZG9udC1idWlsZHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9saWFtbnVnZW50Lm1lL3Bvc3RzL3doYXQteW91LWRvbnQtYnVpbGQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is that the most important product decision is what you don't build, using "document hubs" and "notifications centres" in consumer fintech as examples of features that become bloated, multi-stakeholder projects. These features exist to solve internal/compliance problems (like legacy PDFs and "persistent communications channels") but end up as bad clones of Google Drive or Gmail. The suggestion is to show stakeholders the running costs over years, not just the build cost, to kill such projects.

Engineers should care because it's a playbook for resisting scope creep and avoiding long-term maintenance burdens from features that don't serve users. Be extremely choosy about what you add to a system and be militant about taking things out.

<h2 id="the-curation-problem"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9Db6GPVSMm5DUFpDSy2iDQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW18fGh0dHBzOi8vcnVudGhlYnVzaW5lc3Muc3Vic3RhY2suY29tL3AvdGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW0_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Curation Problem</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9Db6GPVSMm5DUFpDSy2iDQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW18fGh0dHBzOi8vcnVudGhlYnVzaW5lc3Muc3Vic3RhY2suY29tL3AvdGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW0_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: runthebusiness.substack.com ↗</a></p>

The [article](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9Db6GPVSMm5DUFpDSy2iDQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW18fGh0dHBzOi8vcnVudGhlYnVzaW5lc3Muc3Vic3RhY2suY29tL3AvdGhlLWN1cmF0aW9uLXByb2JsZW0_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) warns that product teams using AI to ship faster are falling into a trap of focusing on output metrics, leading to UX clutter and feature bloat. The best predictor of product quality is the number of disciplined, targeted iterations (reps) that compound over time, not the raw volume of shipped features. With AI, more people can prototype and ship, but users' ability to absorb changes is saturated.

Engineers should care because it cautions against the "ship all the things" mindset and argues for curation and disciplined iteration as the path to product-market fit. AI accelerates product development, but teams risk prioritizing speed over meaningful outcomes, leading to feature bloat.

<h2 id="maximizing-return-on-feedback"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/jxOIu077Z_akVVUziQYL6A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8bWF4aW1pemluZy1yZXR1cm4tb24tZmVlZGJhY2t8fGh0dHBzOi8vYXZpdmJlbnlvc2VmLmNvbS9tYXhpbWl6aW5nLXJldHVybi1vbi1mZWVkYmFjay8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Maximizing Return-on-Feedback</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/jxOIu077Z_akVVUziQYL6A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8bWF4aW1pemluZy1yZXR1cm4tb24tZmVlZGJhY2t8fGh0dHBzOi8vYXZpdmJlbnlvc2VmLmNvbS9tYXhpbWl6aW5nLXJldHVybi1vbi1mZWVkYmFjay8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: avivbenyosef.com ↗</a></p>

The [Feedback-Squeezing Protocol](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/jxOIu077Z_akVVUziQYL6A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8bWF4aW1pemluZy1yZXR1cm4tb24tZmVlZGJhY2t8fGh0dHBzOi8vYXZpdmJlbnlvc2VmLmNvbS9tYXhpbWl6aW5nLXJldHVybi1vbi1mZWVkYmFjay8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) starts with the premise that mishandling feedback (dismissing, attacking, or ignoring it) dries up future input. The protocol includes asking: Is it true? Did you realize it too late (and what held the person back)? Were you expecting to hear it from someone else? Addressing the gap between when an issue was first noticed and when you heard about it can reveal cultural misconfigurations.

When you let it be known that someone gave you feedback, and they see how you accepted and thanked the person who gave it, you normalize the behavior and make it safer for others to do the same. Engineers in leadership should care because it's a practical checklist for turning complaints into actionable improvements and preserving a feedback-rich culture.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/BhlHJEvaXke4ueCFZMJpdw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8bm8tY29kZS1pcy1jb2RlLXphcGllcnMtaGVhZGxlc3MtYmV0fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5jb2duaXRpdmVyZXZvbHV0aW9uLmFpL25vLWNvZGUtaXMtY29kZS16YXBpZXItY2VvLXdhZGUtZm9zdGVyLW9uLWhlYWRsZXNzLXRvb2xzLXphcGllci1tY3AtYXV0b21hdGlvbi1iZW5jaC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: cognitiverevolution.ai ↗</a></p>

Zapier CEO [Wade Foster discusses](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/BhlHJEvaXke4ueCFZMJpdw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8bm8tY29kZS1pcy1jb2RlLXphcGllcnMtaGVhZGxlc3MtYmV0fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5jb2duaXRpdmVyZXZvbHV0aW9uLmFpL25vLWNvZGUtaXMtY29kZS16YXBpZXItY2VvLXdhZGUtZm9zdGVyLW9uLWhlYWRsZXNzLXRvb2xzLXphcGllci1tY3AtYXV0b21hdGlvbi1iZW5jaC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) how AI work has consolidated around "daily driver" interfaces like Cursor, Claude Code, and ChatGPT, making headless tools like Zapier MCP (which bring context and data into those harnesses) the winning strategy over forcing users onto a proprietary agent platform. No code is code - the integration layer is the product.
