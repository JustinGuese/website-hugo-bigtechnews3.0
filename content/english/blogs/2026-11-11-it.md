OpenAI just made it dramatically easier for enterprises to buy ChatGPT Enterprise without standing up a new vendor relationship. The product is now in the [AWS Marketplace](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/vO71yT0nVptFUJRYUWbNNQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtaXR8fHxodHRwczovL29wZW5haS5jb20vaW5kZXgvY2hhdGdwdC1lbnRlcnByaXNlLWF3cy1tYXJrZXRwbGFjZS8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), purchasable and manageable through existing AWS accounts and billing. Eligible spend counts toward AWS commitments, and specialized offerings like ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services are available to qualifying customers. For procurement teams that have been fighting to get AI spend past finance, this removes a real obstacle.

The bigger fight this morning is in Washington. [Microsoft and industry groups are lobbying the Trump administration](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/pZGIo5UrEpxo62L9wWRSew/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtaXR8fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5yZXV0ZXJzLmNvbS9idXNpbmVzcy9tZWRpYS10ZWxlY29tL21pY3Jvc29mdC1wcmVzc2VzLXRydW1wLXNjdXR0bGUtZXhwZWN0ZWQtYmFuLWNoaW5lc2UtZGF0YS1jZW50ZXItdGVjaG5vbG9neS0yMDI2LTExLTEwLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) to scuttle a proposed ban on new Chinese optical transceivers in US data centers. Their argument: alternative suppliers simply don't have the capacity to replace Chinese vendors, so a ban would delay data center construction and leave already-purchased AI chips sitting idle. The restrictions are still under consideration, but the stakes for anyone planning 2027 capacity are obvious.
