MiniMax’s M3.1 Flash is the model to watch today: it cuts inference costs by 70%, runs nearly three times faster online, and pushes harder on coding, computer use and security. The company says its agent-focused model, derived from Space Bunny, processed more than 200 trillion tokens anonymously before launch.

That combination matters more than another leaderboard win. Agents burn tokens in loops, make tool calls and wait on sub-second responses, so latency and unit economics decide whether a workflow can run continuously or remains a demo. M3.1 Flash is available through MiniMax’s API, M Plan and MiniMax Code, with weights promised later.

Mercury 3 is trying to change the generation model itself. The diffusion-based LLM reportedly hit 1,068 tokens per second on Nvidia GPUs, alongside a 1-million-token context window. It also completed 50% of a 50-task Mind2Web web-navigation test, beating GPT-6.1-Sol under the same token-volume and agent-step constraints.

[Clairic](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/iPyM1xj03cgn_vjn_yK5sA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjAtYWl8Y2xvdWRmbGFyZXMtY2xhaXJpYy1za2lwcy10b2tlbi1nZW5lcmF0aW9uLWVudGlyZWx5fHxodHRwczovL2Jsb2cuY2xvdWRmbGFyZS5jb20vY2xhaXJpYy1leHRyYWN0aW9uLW1vZGVsP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is the first member of Cloudflare’s “System One” model class, available on Workers AI as `@cf/cloudflare/clairic`. Open weights are planned.

Instead of generating prose, Clairic extracts, calculates, estimates and forecasts structured values from unstructured input. Results are typed and carry confidence scores, while numerical outputs span an 80% range. The architecture can still be wrong, but it removes an entire class of malformed output and free-form hallucination.

For recurring jobs such as reading invoice totals, delivery dates, repair estimates and traffic forecasts, this looks like a cheaper alternative to a frontier-LLM call. The key operational question is whether its confidence and typed outputs map cleanly into existing validation and exception-handling paths.

Meta says the first phase of its [Prometheus supercluster](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/MdT1r-Pw_K0G40Dy3-cIDg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjAtYWl8bWV0YXMtcHJvbWV0aGV1cy1zdXBlcmNsdXN0ZXItaXMtbGVhcm5pbmctdG8tYmUtYm9yaW5nfHxodHRwczovL2VuZ2luZWVyaW5nLmZiLmNvbS8yMDI2LzEwLzE5L2RhdGEtY2VudGVyLWVuZ2luZWVyaW5nL2luc2lkZS1wcm9tZXRoZXVzLW1ldGEtZ2lnYXdhdHQtc2NhbGUtc3VwZXJjbHVzdGVyLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) in New Albany, Ohio, is complete. That last metric is the real infrastructure story. At this scale, the useful unit is not peak FLOPS but the percentage of expensive accelerators kept on useful work. Closed-loop liquid cooling, fiber networking and fleet-level failure handling now matter as much as the chip procurement spreadsheet.

The danger with benchmarks is not that models score badly. It’s that they score well, become training signals, and turn into objectives that no longer represent useful work. [Contrary’s report on climbing the wrong hills](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/LpoENrNT8TVuahPT5hXypg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjAtYWl8YmVuY2htYXJrcy1tYXktYmUtdHJhaW5pbmctdGhlLWluZHVzdHJ5LXRvLW9wdGltaXplLXRoZS13cm9uZy10aGluZ3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9yZXNlYXJjaC5jb250cmFyeS5jb20vcmVwb3J0L2FpLWJlbmNobWFya3MtY2xpbWJpbmctdGhlLXdyb25nLWhpbGxzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) argues that benchmark construction now directly shapes model behavior.

“Benchmaxxing” turns a test into a target. Teams can optimize prompts, tool use, data selection or post-processing until a score improves without making the underlying system more capable. Engineering leaders should keep benchmark gains separate from production evaluations, especially when vendors quote a single result without exposing the harness and token budget.
