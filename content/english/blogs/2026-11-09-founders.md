The data market is immature, and that is about to become your problem. There is no standard pricing, no standard unit, no quality measure - value is moving up the chain by transforming data and getting closer to customer value. Once data gets priced, it enters the capital structure, and deciding what to buy, keep, and sell becomes a core CEO decision, not a procurement line item.

That shift is the day's biggest story because it changes how every AI product gets built. As models get commoditized, the scarce input is novel and private data. Engineers who think they are buying tokens are actually buying access to training material, and the market is splitting into a commoditized "synthetic Walmart of tokens" and a scarce market for the real stuff. The [full essay](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/AsJRUgRBQ8-URteANb3YwQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8fHxodHRwczovLzk5ZC5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC90aGUtZWNvbm9teS1pcy10aGUtY29ycHVzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) argues the market will persist as more models are trained, but only for those who understand the difference.

AI founders have a gross margin problem, and the fix is choosing a unit you control. [The analysis](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/toE720BoQbQI7Y9EqaZpWg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8eW91ci1haS1ncm9zcy1tYXJnaW4taXMtYS1wcmljaW5nLXByb2JsZW18fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3Lm9ubHljZm8uaW8vcC83LXdheXMtdG8taGFuZGxlLXlvdXItYWktZ3Jvc3MtbWFyZ2luP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) from an investor in 700+ companies is blunt: charge by the token, and customers will ask whether you are four times as good as the model you are marking up.

The two poles are GitHub, which bills for tokens used, and Vanta, which eats token costs to drive adoption. Gokul Rajaram's advice: choose a unit your product controls that gets as close as possible to customer value - a personalized email from an AI SDR, not the tokens behind it. Keep measuring token cost per unit, because two support resolutions can bring in the same revenue and cost wildly different amounts. This is now a strategic decision that directly impacts revenue growth and profitability.

The old question was "why wouldn't Google do it?" The new one is [why wouldn't the labs do it?](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Igbn1xhoNckTxygcdUo9BQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8d2h5LXdvdWxkbnQtdGhlLWxhYnMtZG8taXQtdGhleS1hbHJlYWR5LWFyZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly9rb252b3kuYmVlaGlpdi5jb20vcC93aHktd291bGRuLXQtdGhlLWxhYnMtZG8taXQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) Google only threatened companies dependent on search. OpenAI and Anthropic sit on an axis covering most knowledge work, and their core product is knowledge work itself. Anthropic's S-1 names robotics and physical work as target markets.

The implication for founders is strategic: build off-axis. Physical operations, regulated workflows, and proprietary data offer more durable positions against lab competition. If your product is a thin wrapper on a frontier model, you are not a company; you are a feature.

<h2 id="saas-sm-does-not-scale---and-the-numbers-prove-it"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZVe_UkF8F2jkNSWA2huKkw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8c2Fhcy1zbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1zY2FsZS0tLWFuZC10aGUtbnVtYmVycy1wcm92ZS1pdHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9zc29tc3RyZWV0dmVudHVyZXMubWVkaXVtLmNvbS9zYWFzLXMtbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1nZXQtbW9yZS1lZmZpY2llbnQtd2l0aC1zY2FsZS02OTdkNTZlYjI2OTU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SaaS S&amp;M Does Not Scale - And the Numbers Prove It</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZVe_UkF8F2jkNSWA2huKkw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8c2Fhcy1zbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1zY2FsZS0tLWFuZC10aGUtbnVtYmVycy1wcm92ZS1pdHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9zc29tc3RyZWV0dmVudHVyZXMubWVkaXVtLmNvbS9zYWFzLXMtbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1nZXQtbW9yZS1lZmZpY2llbnQtd2l0aC1zY2FsZS02OTdkNTZlYjI2OTU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: blossomstreetventures.medium.com ↗</a></p>

The myth that each sales and marketing dollar buys more revenue as you grow is dead. [A VC firm checked 158 SaaS IPOs](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZVe_UkF8F2jkNSWA2huKkw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8c2Fhcy1zbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1zY2FsZS0tLWFuZC10aGUtbnVtYmVycy1wcm92ZS1pdHx8aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9zc29tc3RyZWV0dmVudHVyZXMubWVkaXVtLmNvbS9zYWFzLXMtbS1kb2VzLW5vdC1nZXQtbW9yZS1lZmZpY2llbnQtd2l0aC1zY2FsZS02OTdkNTZlYjI2OTU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) and found the median fell from $0.71 of new annual revenue per S&M dollar the year before listing to $0.66 in the listing year. The 20 most recent IPOs had nearly twice the revenue at listing and still landed at $0.66.

The firm now treats $0.50 to $0.70 as the normal range for a company that goes public. Some of the strongest listed well below it - Palantir at $0.33, Snowflake at $0.57. The takeaway for founders: do not build a growth model that assumes efficiency you will never get.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9wrbMD1w0QQAegU4RQZ8Hw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW9uZS1wZXJzb24taW5zdGl0dXRpb24tYW5kLXRoZS10b2tlbi1kaWV0fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5jaG9vc2luZ3ZpY3RvcnkuY29tL3AvdGhlLXJpc2Utb2YtdGhlLW9uZS1wZXJzb24taW5zdGl0dXRpb24_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: choosingvictory.com ↗</a></p>

Most white-collar professionals use AI wrong, applying it narrowly to individual content tasks. [The argument](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9wrbMD1w0QQAegU4RQZ8Hw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW9uZS1wZXJzb24taW5zdGl0dXRpb24tYW5kLXRoZS10b2tlbi1kaWV0fHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5jaG9vc2luZ3ZpY3RvcnkuY29tL3AvdGhlLXJpc2Utb2YtdGhlLW9uZS1wZXJzb24taW5zdGl0dXRpb24_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) is that they should build systems and tools for mass-producing knowledge, treating writing problems as engineering problems. A single well-crafted prompt can now elicit a 20-page report exceeding human consultant quality.

The practical path is the [three-stage AI transformation](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ASsGlMXZCYCKKsZ4j0jwAw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLW9uZS1wZXJzb24taW5zdGl0dXRpb24tYW5kLXRoZS10b2tlbi1kaWV0fHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL2VyaWNfc2V1ZmVydC9zdGF0dXMvMjExOTU3MDM3MTUzNjEyNjI5OT91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20): throw an LLM at everything out of fear, then benchmark efficiency and revert what does not work, then go on a token diet. The diet means considering cheaper models like 3.8 Flash instead of frontier ones. It requires custom evals and is time-consuming, but delivers substantial cost savings with little quality trade-off. Only about 20% of consumer app companies have started this sequence, and roughly 10% of those have progressed past stage one. The arbitrage window is open now.
