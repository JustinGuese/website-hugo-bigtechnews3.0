The CFTC has officially initiated rulemaking for [proposed Regulation CTX and Regulation CAM](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/dfPardNMAjB9FsHVzPtN5g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDYtY3J5cHRvfGNmdGMtc2tpcHMtY29uZ3Jlc3Mtd2l0aC1yZWd1bGF0aW9uLWN0eC1hbmQtY2FtfHxodHRwczovL3d3dy50aGVibG9jay5jby9uZXdzL21hcmtldHMvMjAyNi0xMC0wNS1jZnRjLXJ1bGVtYWtpbmctbGV2ZXJhZ2VkLXJldGFpbC1jcnlwdG8tdHJhZGluZy1yZWd1bGF0aW9uLWN0eC1jYW0tNDE3NzAxP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), creating a direct federal pathway for leveraged and margined crypto trading targeted at retail participants. The centerpiece of the package is a brand-new registration class: the Crypto Asset Market (CAM). This structure establishes rules for exchanges offering margin trading on spot commodities, filling an operational vacuum that has kept onshore US venues restricted primarily to fully unleveraged spot markets.

The approach marks a sharp shift in tactical execution. After the comprehensive Clarity Act stalled out in the Senate this September, Chairman Michael Selig acknowledged that the agency cannot unilaterally classify all digital assets or force spot tokens onto CFTC-registered venues without statutory reform. Instead, Regulations CTX and CAM work around that boundary. They regulate the leverage, margin terms, custody, and liquidation engines of the venues themselves, rather than litigating underlying token classifications. 

For trading desks and infrastructure operators, this shifts the onshore compliance roadmap. If adopted, domestic exchanges will not have to wait for sweeping crypto legislation to offer leveraged spot pairs, provided they register as CAM entities and comply with standardized margin call intervals, liquidation waterfalls, and customer asset segregations under CFTC oversight.

From an engineering perspective, this is a clean production benchmark for Uniswap v4’s hook architecture. Traditionally, institutional decentralized finance forced capital into permissioned, walled-garden forks with separate liquidity silos. DeFi Gateway avoids liquidity fragmentation by executing pool-level hooks before and after swaps. These hooks trigger automated Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT) validation checks, verify investor whitelists, and enforce investor-protection limits inside the transaction execution path.

The venue will support traditional stablecoins, tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), and native digital assets. Because the hooks operate at the smart contract level, third-party developers will be able to interface with the pool logic to construct AI-managed yield vaults and retail interfaces that remain compliant with Japanese Financial Services Agency parameters by default.

The Ethereum Foundation released [zkAPI for private, paid developer API access](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZADSNWOEtZCtGkIdpuMldA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDYtY3J5cHRvfGV0aGVyZXVtLWZvdW5kYXRpb24tc2hpcHMtemthcGktZm9yLWlkZW50aXR5LXN0cmlwcGVkLW5ldHdvcmstY2FsbHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwNTc1MzI5MDk3Nzk4MDY4Mi5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). The protocol lets clients interact with metered, paywalled web services without exposing user identity, transaction histories, or consumption patterns to API providers, database logs, or routing intermediaries.

The engineering design decouples billing verification from request traffic. A user deposits ETH into an onchain vault, calculates a zero-knowledge proof attesting to their available collateral, and receives a cryptographic usage allocation. Calls to upstream endpoints present this proof instead of an API key tied to a credit card or billing profile. Upstream proxies can cryptographically verify that the request is funded without learning which account deposited the underlying ETH or reading any offchain identifying information.

The reference deployment is currently live powering OA-chat for LLM inference, but the zero-knowledge verification model is service-agnostic. Up until now, using crypto for metered infrastructure meant either accepting onchain wallet tracking or using centralized prepaid voucher services that preserve IP and payment trails. zkAPI removes payment telemetry at the protocol layer.
