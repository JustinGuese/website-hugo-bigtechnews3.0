Ledger's hardware tampering saga just got bigger. The company says new compromised devices found in Berlin and Dubai are the same incident as the Southeast Asia campaign, and the attackers have upgraded their game - extra hardware now captures your recovery phrase during setup, and one case involves the newer Nano Gen5. Bitquery counts about $1.68M stolen from 16 wallets across the two cities, on top of earlier claims of $102M from 335 wallets in Southeast Asia. Ledger insists there's no flaw in its chip or OS and no confirmed case from ledger.com, but it also hasn't confirmed any loss figures and expects more devices to surface. If you bought a Ledger from anywhere other than the official store, this is the day to check your device's provenance.

Ledger's investigation started with Southeast Asia, but the [new incidents in Berlin and Dubai](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/lE72luJkt_x-LQOSoXxVCA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjAtY3J5cHRvfHRoZS10YW1wZXJpbmctY2FtcGFpZ24tbm93LXNwYW5zLXRocmVlLWNvbnRpbmVudHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRoZWJsb2NrLmNvL25ld3Mvd2ViMy8yMDI2LTExLTE4LWxlZGdlci1uZXctaGFyZHdhcmUtaW5jaWRlbnRzLWdlcm1hbnktdWFlLTQ0MzUzMD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) confirm this is a coordinated, multi-region operation. The attack vector is the same: tampered devices shipped through authorized resellers, not the official ledger.com store. The upgrade is the extra hardware that captures the recovery phrase during setup - meaning even users who did everything right during initialization are exposed.

Two users claim a combined $604,000 in losses from a Dubai-authorized reseller. Bitquery's on-chain data shows about $1.68M leaving 16 wallets in Dubai and Berlin. The discrepancy between claimed and confirmed losses is worth noting: Ledger hasn't confirmed any loss amounts, and the earlier $102M figure from Southeast Asia remains unverified. What's clear is that the threat model for hardware wallets has changed - a tampered device can now exfiltrate your seed phrase before you even type it in. If you're running a custody operation or managing a treasury, this is a reminder that supply-chain integrity is as critical as the cryptography itself.

For engineers building on this stack, the key detail is delivery-versus-payment: the protocol atomically settles both legs of a trade, eliminating the settlement risk that plagues traditional markets. The yield market allows BTC holders to earn returns without handing over their asset, settling on Bitcoin and secured by 100% of Bitcoin's proof-of-work security budget.

The numbers: bonds target 3% annual yield paid in BTC from miners' Proof of Transfer commitments. The first two bonds drew 735 BTC, with Bullish staking in Bond 3 and Anchorage offering custody. This addresses the core problem of Bitcoin's unproductive capital, offering a trust-minimized alternative to failed lending platforms like Celsius and BlockFi.
