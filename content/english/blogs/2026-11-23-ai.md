OpenAI dropped the internal numbers nobody else publishes. In a post arguing that perplexity - cross-entropy loss on held-out data - is the single best metric for tracking AI progress, the company released pretraining perplexity measurements for models from GPT-3.5 onward. Lower perplexity tracks a stunning range of capabilities, from GPT-2's coherent paragraphs to substantially more. This is the first time OpenAI has opened its training curves, and it establishes a shared, quantitative basis for comparing model developers.

The stakes are bigger than leaderboard bragging. If perplexity becomes the agreed-upon yardstick, it gives regulators and safety bodies a concrete number to hang standards on. The catch: OpenAI just told every competitor exactly how much headroom it has left. Whether that transparency survives contact with the market is another question.

[Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3x8rY7K1WceGitjyBnSo1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjMtYWl8aW5rbGluZy0yLW1pbmktbG9vcGluZy10by10aGUtdG9wLW9mLW9wZW4tbW9kZWxzfHxodHRwczovL3RoaW5raW5nbWFjaGluZXMuYWkvbmV3cy9pbmtsaW5nLTItbWluaS8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), a 26.4B-total-parameter transformer with just 3.4B active. The trick is architecture: it runs its middle layers three times per token, trading compute for reasoning depth. It leads every open-weights model under 100B parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%).

The practical detail for engineers is the ~20GB memory footprint. Weights are native NVFP4 (last four layers MXFP8), which means this runs offline on a decent consumer laptop. The looping also extends the no-CoT time horizon from 16 to 37 seconds without increasing agentic sabotage risk, which is a genuinely useful property for monitoring. If you need a coding model that fits in local memory and beats the cloud-hosted competition, this is the benchmark to beat.

This is a different decoding paradigm - if you're running inference at scale, the throughput numbers justify a serious look.
