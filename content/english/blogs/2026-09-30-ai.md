[Devin is now 30-40% cheaper](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/OKzaC-lka6-PlU5vLK9QRw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vZGV2aW4uYWkvYmxvZy9tb3JlLWVmZmljaWVudC1kZXZpbj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) in Fusion and Normal mode, up to 70% cheaper in Devin Review, thanks to new models like SWE-2 and harness improvements. Fusion leads FrontierCode 1.1 with a 68.8 score at $0.60 per task. The lesson: model independence and smarter tool batching are what actually cut agentic coding costs.

[Baseten partnered with OpenAI](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/MHoTtR8yi60u-5SuVahCPg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmJhc2V0ZW4uY28vYmxvZy9iYXNldGVuLW9wZW5haS1wYXJ0bmVyc2hpcC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) to serve open models natively via Codex and the Responses API. Enterprise customers can use existing OpenAI commitments for Baseten-served open models, enabling multi-model routing. That's a real shift — mixing open and closed models within one contract.

[Cohere's Embed 5](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/S852DHhTRCRo8hD2f5097w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vZG9jcy5jb2hlcmUuY29tL2NoYW5nZWxvZy9lbWJlZC12NS5tZD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) shows major gains over Embed 4 on visually rich documents, financial filings, parsed PDFs, code, and multilingual retrieval. If you're doing RAG on documents that aren't clean text, re-benchmark.

[Liquid AI released Pipette](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/MkPfBy2fnSF6Ur1Es4Lebw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwNTAwMzQ3MjMzMjY5Mzg2OS5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), an open-source (Apache 2.0) model evaluation suite for on-device AI. It compares models as model + quantization + runtime + device, covering 35 model classes and 7 llama.cpp quant levels. For anyone shipping on-device models, this is the reproducible benchmark tool you've been missing.

[OpenAI's GPT-Live-1](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/aoc5PRM6u3KBEKT3Fi1FxQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwNTAwMzMxODkxNzY5Nzg3My5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is in the API, enabling voice agents that listen and speak simultaneously, distinguishing speech from background noise and handling mid-conversation interruptions in one model. Lower latency, fewer handoffs — worth evaluating for voice apps.

And the [FIG benchmark](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/lNsoO5Ox8R80nJOPDUMkrw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMzAtYWl8dGhlLXJlc3QtZGV2aW4tYmFzZXRlbi1jb2hlcmUtYW5kLXRoZS1tb2RlbC16b298fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmZpZy5pbmMvYmxvZy9hc3RyYS1vcHVzLTUtNS1hbmQtb3RoZXItZnJvbnRpZXItbW9kZWxzLWRlbW9uc3RyYXRlLWphZ2dlZC1wZXJmb3JtYW5jZS1hY3Jvc3Mtc290YS1hZ2VudGljLXRhc2tzLWZyb20td2ViLWJyb3dzaW5nLXRvLXJvYm90aWNzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) confirms what everyone suspected: there's no clear leader across agentic tasks. High variance in model-task fit means you benchmark per use case, not per model family.
