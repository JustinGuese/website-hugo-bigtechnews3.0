The New York Stock Exchange has spent the past year testing [Avalanche technology](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/B8LvpHSG04StN23dqcpngg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfG55c2Utc3BlbnQtYS15ZWFyLXRlc3RpbmctYXZhbGFuY2hlLWZvci10b2tlbml6ZWQtZXF1aXRpZXN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRoZWJsb2NrLmNvL25ld3MvZWNvc3lzdGVtcy8yMDI2LTA5LTE4LWF2YS1sYWJzLXByZXNpZGVudC1zYXlzLW55c2Utc3BlbnQtYS15ZWFyLXRlc3RpbmctYXZhbGFuY2hlLXRlY2hub2xvZ3ktdG9rZW5pemF0aW9uLXBsYW5zLTQxNTUwOT91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) and probing its economics as it builds a platform for onchain trading and settlement of tokenized US equities and ETFs. Ava Labs President Charley Cooper described a "close working relationship" with NYSE parent ICE but stopped short of saying Avalanche had been selected. ICE's Michael Blaugrund said the firm remains "very engaged" in evaluating blockchain platforms.

For engineers, the interesting bit is what "testing economics" means operationally. Settlement finality, validator costs, and per-transaction fees at NYSE scale are nothing like a DeFi app. If Avalanche's subnets can't hit the throughput and cost profile ICE needs for lit-order trading, all the relationship goodwill in the world won't make it production. The fact that this is still a test after a year tells you the bar is high.

[Visa is moving to stop payment processors](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/-ElJgZde4o4UzOYqbP3zXA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfHZpc2EtY2xvc2VzLXRoZS1tZW1lY29pbi1yZXdhcmRzLWxvb3Bob2xlfHxodHRwczovL3d3dy50aGVibG9jay5jby9uZXdzL3JlZ3VsYXRpb24vMjAyNi0wOS0xOS12aXNhLXRvLWNsb3NlLWNyb3NzbWludC1tZW1lY29pbi1yZXdhcmRzLWxvb3Bob2xlLWZvbGxvd2luZy10aGUtYmxvY2staW52ZXN0aWdhdGlvbi1yZXBvcnQtNDE1ODY2P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) from categorizing memecoin purchases under merchant code 5815, the "digital media" category that lets credit card buyers earn ordinary points or cash back. The change follows The Block's investigation and a direct warning to Checkout.com, Crossmint's processor. Visa's grace period is expected to end next week, after which purchases must be processed under standard crypto transaction rules.

Crossmint already pulled tokens GENIUS and DEGEN from its Apple Pay checkout after The Block questioned their classification as memecoins. New York Attorney General Letitia James' office said it is looking into the matter. Mastercard has not disclosed whether it will follow Visa's lead. For anyone running a payments stack, this is a reminder that merchant category codes are a regulatory surface, not just a billing field. If your processor lets you pick the MCC, expect that flexibility to shrink.

The first [LLM inference run executed as pure onchain Solana transactions](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/NZVfwWhuEJQ6iKbyngpJ8w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfGZpcnN0LWxsbS1ydW5zLWVudGlyZWx5LW9uY2hhaW4tdmlhLXNvbGFuYS1zdm18fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwMDQ1MzE1MDczNzk2MTA4OS5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), with model weights, activations, attention calculations, and token outputs each processed as standard SVM program calls. No trusted black-box attestation - the inference is an auditable, verifiable ledger entry. That breaks with most current AI agent and oracle architectures, where inference results are attested after the fact rather than proven in place.

The operational question is cost. Per-inference gas on SVM is nontrivial, and the thread doesn't pretend otherwise. But if the pattern compresses to practical levels, it changes the trust model for AI agents holding funds or signing transactions. You no longer need to trust an offchain model provider; you can verify the exact computation that produced a given output. That is a meaningful shift for anyone who has ever debugged an agent that hallucinated a transaction.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/WmbXSGg8HkJozrIsptUOUg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfHBvc3QtcXVhbnR1bS1zZWN1cml0eS1pcy1ub3ctYS1wcm9jdXJlbWVudC1wcm9ibGVtfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL3BvZGRvdG5ldHdvcmsvc3RhdHVzLzIxMDA5NzExNjc2MTE4MDU3OTk_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: x.com ↗</a></p>

Google's 2026 quantum computing progress report set a [2029 PQ readiness target](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/WmbXSGg8HkJozrIsptUOUg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfHBvc3QtcXVhbnR1bS1zZWN1cml0eS1pcy1ub3ctYS1wcm9jdXJlbWVudC1wcm9ibGVtfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL3BvZGRvdG5ldHdvcmsvc3RhdHVzLzIxMDA5NzExNjc2MTE4MDU3OTk_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), converting the migration from deferred planning to current procurement. NIST has finalized ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), and SLH-DSA (FIPS 205). The "harvest now, decrypt later" attack vector is operational today, with encrypted traffic being recorded against the day a fault-tolerant quantum computer exists.

Blockchain infrastructure faces the same ECC replacement pressure as traditional PKI. ECDSA and EdDSA wallet key material and transaction signatures are exactly the kind of long-lived secrets that will be harvested and broken. The migration concentrates in four areas: hardware security modules and trusted execution environments, crypto-agile embedded devices, machine-to-machine authentication, and the actual signature schemes on chain. If your chain doesn't have a plan for post-quantum signatures, you are assuming Q-Day is further out than your users' funds will live.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/PmSuocnz_OUhwFDzE8yiRQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfHdoZW4tZXZlcnktdHJhZGVyLXVzZXMtYWktbm9ib2R5LWJlYXRzLXRoZS1tYXJrZXR8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwMTMxODM1NDU4OTg2NDI3Ny5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: threadreaderapp.com ↗</a></p>

When all retail traders use the same LLMs with the same basic [prompts, the result is herding rather than efficiency gains](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/PmSuocnz_OUhwFDzE8yiRQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMjEtY3J5cHRvfHdoZW4tZXZlcnktdHJhZGVyLXVzZXMtYWktbm9ib2R5LWJlYXRzLXRoZS1tYXJrZXR8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEwMTMxODM1NDU4OTg2NDI3Ny5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). Classical EMH assumes independent actors with diverse information; homogeneous AI agents break that assumption. Momentum strategies then amplify consensus before it forms, creating crowded trades that unwind together.

For quant engineers, this is an argument that alpha will shift to data and execution speed, not better reasoning. If everyone's model says the same thing, the edge is in who can front-run the consensus. That is a structural change in how retail alpha is made, and it is happening whether or not the market has priced it in.
