<h2 id="solana-replaces-vote-transactions-with-alpenglow-targeting-150-ms-finality">Solana replaces vote transactions with Alpenglow, targeting 150 ms finality</h2>

Solana activated its Alpenglow upgrade on Wednesday, deploying the most substantial consensus-level overhaul to the network since mainnet launched in 2020. The upgrade replaces the legacy Tower BFT consensus mechanism’s vote-as-transaction design. Previously, validators broadcast their consensus votes as standard on-chain transactions, which were batched into successive blocks and took several seconds to reach irreversible finality. Under Alpenglow, validators exchange votes out-of-band directly with each other and aggregate approvals into cryptographic certificates, allowing a block to finalize in one or two rounds.

The engineering target is roughly 150 milliseconds for block finality under normal networking conditions. The validator handover executed in roughly 900 ms, with 446 of 654 active validators casting votes on the transition block. For backend teams, end-user wallets and fee models do not change, but API consumers and telemetry scrapers will see immediate anomalies. Because validator votes no longer hit the ledger as state-modifying transactions, high-level TPS trackers will show a severe drop in aggregate transactions per second, despite user traffic remaining identical. Anza warned that while consensus finality will trigger in ~150 ms for participating validators, propagation lag means downstream RPC nodes and observer infrastructure may register finality slightly later. 

<h2 id="starknet-votes-935-to-research-breaking-away-into-a-post-quantum-layer-1">Starknet votes 93.5% to research breaking away into a post-quantum Layer 1</h2>

The Starknet community closed a seven-day Snapshot governance poll on Thursday with over 93.5% of voting power - spanning more than 2,800 unique addresses - approving research into decoupling from Ethereum. The proposal envisions transforming Starknet from an Ethereum validity rollup into an independent, post-quantum-secure Layer 1 blockchain. Core developer entity StarkWare abstained from the vote, leaving the mandate entirely to community token holders. 

The rationale is architectural. Starknet utilizes STARKs and hash-based cryptography, which are inherently post-quantum secure at the execution and account levels. Users can implement quantum-resistant signatures inside their smart contracts right now without touching the underlying L2 protocol. However, as long as Starknet settles state roots and relies on data availability posted to Ethereum, it inherits the quantum vulnerability of Ethereum's existing cryptographic curve (ECDSA/BLS). The bridge remains an unfixable attack vector if a quantum adversary compromises the L1 state. A formal draft whitepaper exploring sovereign consensus and data availability is scheduled for November, with a binding STRK migration vote required before any code ships.

The ve(3,3) decentralized exchanges Aerodrome (Base) and Velodrome (Optimism) officially completed their protocol merger on October 21, launching as [Aero](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/5TJdMWRD3WyZGPbhNFzlNg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjMtY3J5cHRvfGFlcm9kcm9tZS1hbmQtdmVsb2Ryb21lLXVuaWZ5LWludG8tYWVyby1hY3Jvc3Mtc2V2ZW4tY2hhaW5zfHxodHRwczovL2Flcm8ueHl6L2FydGljbGVzL2Flcm8tbGF1bmNoZXMtdG9kYXkvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ). The unified Automated Market Maker went live simultaneously across seven EVM networks, including Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, and Robinhood Chain, consolidating governance and emissions beneath a single AERO token. The protocol launched without private investor allocations or fresh token sales, having distributed the consolidated token supply to legacy holders based on trailing 52-week protocol revenues - a distribution formula that yielded 94.5% to Aerodrome participants and 5.5% to Velodrome.

Architecturally, the merged protocol ships "Slipstream V3," an iteration of concentrated liquidity coupled with automated MEV recapture mechanisms. It also rolls out single-transaction "metaswaps" that fold bridging, cross-chain path routing, and swap execution into a unified call. The economic engine funnels 100% of trading fee revenue directly to sAERO stakers. To curb structural emissions dilution, the engine imposes an LP incentive cap indexed directly to projected pool fee generation, programmatically canceling emissions for pools that fail to clear the revenue threshold.

<h2 id="xmtp-shuts-down-hosted-messaging-network-pivots-to-bare-metal-rust-stack">XMTP shuts down hosted messaging network, pivots to bare-metal Rust stack</h2>

XMTP Labs announced it will permanently decommission its hosted communication cluster in early 2027. The project is walking away from its original protocol design, conceding that coupling private messaging infrastructure to Web3 wallet identity, on-chain state, and a speculative token architecture failed to achieve enterprise developer traction. The team confirmed that no native token will ever launch for the project.

Instead, XMTP is open-sourcing a self-hosted, sovereign messaging backend rewritten by CTO Nick Molnar. The entire server component has been consolidated into a single bare-metal Rust binary that handles cryptographic ratchets, payload attachments, push notifications, identity handles, and outbound signed webhooks. Client libraries have been stripped of mandatory crypto wallet dependencies, letting engineers deploy end-to-end encrypted messaging services for both human users and automated autonomous agents. Self-hosted instances operate fully sandboxed by default, removing any platform dependency on XMTP Labs' existing infrastructure.

<h2 id="coinbase-endorses-atkins-led-sec-framework-for-token-disclosures">Coinbase endorses Atkins-led SEC framework for token disclosures</h2>

Coinbase Chief Policy Officer Faryar Shirzad filed an official comment letter backing "Regulation Crypto Assets," a formal regulatory package proposed under SEC Chair Paul Atkins. The proposal outlines a disclosure-centric registration apparatus designed to allow crypto tokens to trade on regulated domestic platforms without triggering enforcement actions under legacy 1933 Act securities registrations. 

Shirzad’s public filing highlights a stark operational departure from the enforcement-led posture of the previous Gary Gensler administration. If codified, Regulation Crypto Assets would give token founders a deterministic checklist for retail secondary distributions, standardizing requirements around smart contract audit disclosures, distribution schedules, and insider lockup reporting. For engineering and protocol legal teams, the rule would replace ambiguous Howey test litigation with concrete reporting milestones.

The integration of onchain execution with enterprise machine learning is running into hard operational limits around data privacy. While decentralized compute marketplaces like Gensyn, Ritual, and Hyperbolic continue expanding distributed GPU capacity, unencrypted inference pipelines expose raw weights, system prompts, and training inputs to bare-metal host operators. To solve this, operators are racing to establish a [confidential inference](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/CVA1O_nH2Nc2yIphvgTCzw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjMtY3J5cHRvfG9uY2hhaW4tYWktaW5mcmFzdHJ1Y3R1cmUtY29uZmlkZW50aWFsLWluZmVyZW5jZS1hbmQtYWdlbnQtcm91dGluZ3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aHJlYWRyZWFkZXJhcHAuY29tL3RocmVhZC8yMTEyNzc4MzA2NDMzMTg4MDIxLmh0bWw_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) baseline, leveraging Hardware Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs), Zero-Knowledge proofs (zkML), and Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) to guarantee compute integrity on untrusted hardware. Node runners executing models inside verified hardware enclaves are already pricing confidential inference as a high-margin tier above commoditized bare-metal FLOPs.

Higher up the stack, AI agents are increasingly dominating execution volume. Metrics from [Coinbase for Agents](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/_lRXP6tbwlXr_Glv0T1CbA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjMtY3J5cHRvfG9uY2hhaW4tYWktaW5mcmFzdHJ1Y3R1cmUtY29uZmlkZW50aWFsLWluZmVyZW5jZS1hbmQtYWdlbnQtcm91dGluZ3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aHJlYWRyZWFkZXJhcHAuY29tL3RocmVhZC8yMTEzMDgwODI0NTE4MjkxNzM4Lmh0bWw_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) showed another record high in weekly transactional activity, with automated model routing showing clear market concentration: Grok Bot drove 45.7% of volume, standalone Grok processed 29.7%, custom CLI tools captured 12.4%, and Anthropic’s Claude suite took 7.6%. As these agents begin settling consumer workflows, payments rails are penalizing them. Because automated agents rely on programmatic virtual credit cards issued to software companies, interchange networks like Visa and Mastercard routinely categorize their queries as [commercial card transactions](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3YNdQ2HEs737LSgbfLVa9Q/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjMtY3J5cHRvfG9uY2hhaW4tYWktaW5mcmFzdHJ1Y3R1cmUtY29uZmlkZW50aWFsLWluZmVyZW5jZS1hbmQtYWdlbnQtcm91dGluZ3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aHJlYWRyZWFkZXJhcHAuY29tL3RocmVhZC8yMTEzMTk2MTEzMjg5MjEyMDkzLmh0bWw_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) - forcing autonomous agents to pay the highest fee tier in the legacy payments ecosystem and accelerating the demand for low-latency crypto settlement rails.
