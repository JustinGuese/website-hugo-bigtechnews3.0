Anthropic's [guide to always-on agents](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/e74gldCf9ztFoysJEtReBQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTYtaXR8YnVpbGRpbmctYWx3YXlzLW9uLWFnZW50c3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9jbGF1ZGUuY29tL3Jlc291cmNlcy9hcnRpY2xlcy9idWlsZGluZy1hbHdheXMtb24tYWdlbnRzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) lays out the architecture for stateful, proactive agents that run indefinitely across sessions.
