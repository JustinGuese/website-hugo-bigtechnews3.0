[Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/YByb_2tBM_XWE4m5xwKVDg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTAtYWl8Y2xhdWRlLWZhYmxlLTUyLWFuZC1teXRob3MtNTItc2FtZS1icmFpbi1kaWZmZXJlbnQtbGVhc2h8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmFudGhyb3BpYy5jb20vY2xhdWRlLWZhYmxlLWFuZC1teXRob3MtNS0yP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), two versions of the same underlying model with different safety guardrails. Fable gets the standard alignment treatment; Mythos has reduced cyber and biology safeguards and requires an application for access. If you're building for regulated industries, you'll want Fable. If you're doing red-team work or security research, Mythos is the one to request.

The engineering story is speed and price. Both models generate outputs up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions, with pricing at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. There's also an optional fast mode at $16/$80 per million tokens that speeds generation by up to 2.5x. That's a meaningful lever for latency-sensitive agentic workloads, but it doubles your token bill, so do the math before flipping it on.

The models also claim improved coding, reasoning, and vision capabilities. The pricing puts them squarely against GPT-6 Luna and the Haiku line, and the 30% speedup is the kind of thing that shows up in your p95 latency charts immediately.

If you're on GCP, the interesting question is which of these maps to your workload, because the price-performance curves are going to diverge sharply.
