A novel L2 deployment (Chain ID 42069000, active since late September) has introduced an [OP Stack rollup that replaces ECDSA](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/AnQ9smqZ3NWPgNdOHv5vAg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjYtY3J5cHRvfGFuLW9wLXN0YWNrLXJvbGx1cC1ydW5uaW5nLWVudGlyZWx5LW9uLWhhc2gtY2hhaW5zfHxodHRwczovL2V0aHJlc2Vhci5jaC90L2EtaGFzaC1vbmx5LXJvbGx1cC1vcC1zdGFjay1sMi1hY2NvdW50cy1hcy1sYW1wb3J0LXN0eWxlLWhhc2gtY2hhaW5zLW5vLWVjZHNhLWluc2lkZS10aGUtbDIvMjYxNzI_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) with Lamport-style keccak-256 hash chains for all transaction validation inside the network. Deployed by the founder of bb84 payments, the chain completely eliminates curve-based signatures from the execution environment to build post-quantum resistance targeting roughly $2^{128}$ Grover work operations.

Account creation requires generating a secret seed that expands into a 1,000-step keccak-256 chain. Transaction execution relies on custom EIP-2718 envelopes: type `0x7C` acts as a commitment step, and type `0x7F` executes the reveal phase by publishing the next preimage, with a `NullifierTree` predeploy tracking consumed states. The rollup activated a "first commit wins" rule on October 21 to mitigate sequencer front-running. The architectural catch is severe: ECDSA is still required to bridge assets on L1, the sequencer is centralized, interactive fault proofs are absent, and because user accounts are stateful preimages, desynchronizing the local chain index permanently bricks wallet funds.
