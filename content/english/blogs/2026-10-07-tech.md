Apple is finally expanding its first-party smart home perimeter, but it is not manufacturing the hardware alone. The company has partnered with LG to co-develop an entire fleet of peripheral devices - including a smart doorbell, a door lock, and a thermostat - designed to sit directly alongside a new central smart home hub. 

The move signals a pragmatic shift for Cupertino. Rather than agonizing over low-margin hardware manufacturing cycles for commodity home accessories, Apple is leveraging LG’s supply chain while asserting software dominance through protocol standards.

At the same time, the broader AI layer is splintering into extremes: on one end, OpenAI is publishing solutions to hundreds of unsolved pure-math theorems using unreleased reasoning models; on the other, production engineering is discarding raw token generation entirely in favor of lightweight, sub-second decision endpoints.

Thermal and mechanical upgrades to existing reactor cores cost billions, but they require significantly less regulatory overhead and can clear within five years. Industry projections indicate these uprates will pull an additional 6,000 to 8,000 megawatts of baseload capacity onto the grid specifically earmarked for hyperscale data centers. It represents a massive capital shift: computing clusters are no longer being provisioned based on where fiber is cheapest, but where gigawatt-scale interconnect agreements can close the fastest.

Generative text is increasingly being evicted from synchronous application paths. OpenAI rolled out the public beta of its [Decisions API](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/GsuRJ-iT1vHCh4zZ8T_x0w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDctdGVjaHx0aGUtZW5kLW9mLXN0cmVhbWluZy10ZXh0LXRoZS1kZWNpc2lvbi1tb2RlbC1nb2xkLXJ1c2h8fGh0dHBzOi8vZGV2ZWxvcGVycy5vcGVuYWkuY29tL2FwaS9kb2NzL2d1aWRlcy9kZWNpc2lvbnM_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), powered exclusively by `gpt-6-luna` on the `POST /v1/decisions` endpoint. Instead of emitting tokens, it ingests multimodal data and returns strict typed answers - boolean predicates, confidence probabilities, rubric-based scores, or discrete enum picks - roughly 10 times faster than the Responses API.

As Swapnil Talekar writes in [The Decision Model Gold Rush](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/9NnjC5phEjtgVFI032hy_g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDctdGVjaHx0aGUtZW5kLW9mLXN0cmVhbWluZy10ZXh0LXRoZS1kZWNpc2lvbi1tb2RlbC1nb2xkLXJ1c2h8fGh0dHBzOi8vc3dhcG5pbHRhbGVrYXIuc3Vic3RhY2suY29tL3AvdGhlLWRlY2lzaW9uLW1vZGVsLWdvbGQtcnVzaD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), this marks a major architectural transition across the ecosystem. When TypeSafe launched its Jev model, it captured 13% of Vercel’s AI Gateway traffic within 24 hours, prompting rapid releases like Amazon’s open-weight Strands Decider 2B (built on Qwen3.5-2B) and Cloudflare’s Clef. Using a 70B-parameter conversational model to run a routing decision or content check is dead architecture; dedicated evaluators optimize the latency, cost, and reliability profile.

Native JPEG XL (`.jxl`) decoding is officially arriving in Chrome 155, per the [Chrome Developer Blog](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/lPagZ7Isn0c50VseiVZeXQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDctdGVjaHxydXN0LWJyaW5ncy1tZW1vcnktc2FmZS1qcGVnLXhsLXRvLWNocm9tZS13aGlsZS1kbnMtcm9vdC1yb3RhdGVzfHxodHRwczovL2RldmVsb3Blci5jaHJvbWUuY29tL2Jsb2cvanBlZy14bC1pbi1jaHJvbWU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t). The format brings 30% to 50% better compression over legacy JPEG, along with native HDR and bit-for-bit lossless JPEG transcoding.
