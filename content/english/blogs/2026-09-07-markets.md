<h2 id="agent-identity-is-no-longer-contested">Agent identity is no longer contested</h2>
Okta, Auth0, and Descope independently shipped Cross App Access implementations between Aug 24 and Sep 1, and Okta's Agent SSO is now GA with Claude, Slack, Notion, and Datadog integrations. When three vendors converge on the same pattern inside eight days, the pattern has won. The second-order beneficiary is every agent platform that gets SSO for free; the loser is whoever hoped to own agent identity themselves. We stay positive on OKTA at conviction 3 - this is the standard coalescing around their implementation, which is worth more than any single product launch.

<h2 id="the-model-agnostic-layer-is-where-enterprise-money-is-going">The model-agnostic layer is where enterprise money is going</h2>
Snowflake's CoCo (ex-Cortex Code) hit 9,100 customer accounts, up 28%-plus quarter-over-quarter, running Anthropic, OpenAI, and open-weight models including DeepSeek-V4-Flash and GLM-5.3. Read that carefully: enterprise buyers are actively diversifying after Anthropic's price increases, and the intermediary capturing that behavior is Snowflake, not the model vendors. GitHub's Project HydraFusion points the same direction - runtime multi-model orchestration matching Opus 5 quality at lower cost means Copilot's coding quality is no longer hostage to any single frontier model. The commoditization pressure on frontier APIs is now visible in three separate data points this week: Chinese open-weight models priced 60-90% below Western APIs, Snowflake's multi-model routing, and GitHub's orchestration layer.
