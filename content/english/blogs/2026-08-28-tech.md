Anthropic has spent the last year teaching Claude to write code, browse the web and call APIs. Today it taught the model to drive a laser. The [Model Hardware Standard](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/jYBuyBNLWzpukNbZe5JbRg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHx8fGh0dHBzOi8vYXJzdGVjaG5pY2EuY29tL2FpLzIwMjYvMDgvYW50aHJvcGljcy1uZXctaGFyZHdhcmUtc3RhbmRhcmQtbGV0cy1haS1hZ2VudHMtY29udHJvbC10aGUtcGh5c2ljYWwtd29ybGQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), shipping as a research preview aimed at scientific labs, is a set of standardized drivers plus a common data format that lets AI agents talk to microscopes, cameras and other lab instruments without bespoke glue code. Pair it with the Model Context Protocol and an agent can update instrument parameters mid-run, recover from hardware faults, or just take the wheel in plain English. Anthropic says the goal is to compress weeks or months of experimental setup into "hours or minutes," and the bet is that lab robotics, like software before it, eventually ships with drivers that already know how to talk to a competent agent.

Addy Osmani [argues](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/nIzjUh76hRZ8w_EMGpTCWg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHx5b3VyLWFnZW50c21kLWhhcy1hLWhhbGYtbGlmZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly9hZGR5by5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC9hdWRpdC15b3VyLWFnZW50LWZpbGVzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) that agent configuration files, the CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and skills directory entries that steer coding agents, decay. Calvin French-Owen [writes](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Nv13SldlRb-AZzfLH7uFTw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxzbWFsbC1tb2RlbHMtY3Jvc3NlZC1hLWxpbmV8fGh0dHBzOi8vY2Fsdi5pbmZvL3NtYWxsLW1vZGVscy1oYXZlLWFycml2ZWQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) that gpt-5.6-luna runs at roughly 100 tokens per second and turns a research thread that used to cost about $1 into something that costs tens of cents. <p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DAm8ZkRT2ILwu2_z4Vem1g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxzdHJpcGUtcGFpZC03Yi1mb3ItYW4tYWktYmFua3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aGVmaW5hbmNpYWxlbmdpbmVlci5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC93aHktZGlkLXN0cmlwZS1hY3F1aXJlLWFuLWFpLW1vZGVsP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: thefinancialengineer.substack.com ↗</a></p>

The [acquisition](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DAm8ZkRT2ILwu2_z4Vem1g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxzdHJpcGUtcGFpZC03Yi1mb3ItYW4tYWktYmFua3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aGVmaW5hbmNpYWxlbmdpbmVlci5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC93aHktZGlkLXN0cmlwZS1hY3F1aXJlLWFuLWFpLW1vZGVsP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) closed on August 16 at more than $7B, roughly 5x OpenRouter's $1.3B Series B from three months earlier. OpenRouter is misread as a thin proxy; it actually sits between 80+ model providers, independent inference vendors and 10M+ developers, routing over 10 trillion tokens a day across 400+ models. The author's case is that this is marketplace infrastructure, not plumbing, and that Stripe's pitch is to become the bank every AI company is currently forced to build for itself. Stripe's DNA is marketplace settlement, so the fit is closer than "payments company buys a router" sounds.

<h2 id="agent-swarms-are-not-databases"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Qkcrs1UPGvLBhe8cekqhLg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxhZ2VudC1zd2FybXMtYXJlLW5vdC1kYXRhYmFzZXN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRyeWNocm9tYS5jb20vZW5naW5lZXJpbmcvdHJhbnNhY3Rpb25zP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Agent swarms are not databases</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Qkcrs1UPGvLBhe8cekqhLg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxhZ2VudC1zd2FybXMtYXJlLW5vdC1kYXRhYmFzZXN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRyeWNocm9tYS5jb20vZW5naW5lZXJpbmcvdHJhbnNhY3Rpb25zP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: trychroma.com ↗</a></p>

Chroma's Robert Escriva [argues](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/Qkcrs1UPGvLBhe8cekqhLg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxhZ2VudC1zd2FybXMtYXJlLW5vdC1kYXRhYmFzZXN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnRyeWNocm9tYS5jb20vZW5naW5lZXJpbmcvdHJhbnNhY3Rpb25zP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) that multi-agent systems are distributed systems problems, and borrowing transaction semantics from databases is the wrong move. Agent "transactions" run for minutes and cost real money to retry, so abort-and-restart discards more useful work than it saves. Git-based conflict resolution is also wrong for agent-written prose, since natural-language edits get summarised, reorganised and rewritten in ways that do not line up with line-based diffs. The metric Chroma settled on is "goodput": the share of paid reasoning that survives rather than being thrown away on abort. The implication is that the next round of agent infrastructure looks more like a distributed runtime than a database with extra steps.

<h2 id="hypergrowth-is-still-accelerating"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ANqQIQ402dcoiDOd7v1cWw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoeXBlcmdyb3d0aC1pcy1zdGlsbC1hY2NlbGVyYXRpbmd8fGh0dHBzOi8vZXBvY2hhaS5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC9hbi11cGRhdGUtb24tYWlzLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LW51bWJlcj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hypergrowth is still accelerating</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ANqQIQ402dcoiDOd7v1cWw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoeXBlcmdyb3d0aC1pcy1zdGlsbC1hY2NlbGVyYXRpbmd8fGh0dHBzOi8vZXBvY2hhaS5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC9hbi11cGRhdGUtb24tYWlzLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LW51bWJlcj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: epochai.substack.com ↗</a></p>

Epoch AI [updates](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ANqQIQ402dcoiDOd7v1cWw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoeXBlcmdyb3d0aC1pcy1zdGlsbC1hY2NlbGVyYXRpbmd8fGh0dHBzOi8vZXBvY2hhaS5zdWJzdGFjay5jb20vcC9hbi11cGRhdGUtb24tYWlzLW1vc3QtaW1wb3J0YW50LW51bWJlcj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) the revenue run-rate chart that has become the single most-watched number in tech. OpenAI tripled from $13B to $40B in the last year. Anthropic went from $1B to $9B in 2025, then more than tripled again in Q1 2026 to a reported $65B by end of July. <h2 id="harness-engineering-the-missing-layer"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZODpDh8WBDllSFNa1L_qNg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoYXJuZXNzLWVuZ2luZWVyaW5nLXRoZS1taXNzaW5nLWxheWVyfHxodHRwczovL2hhYml0YXQtdGhpbmtpbmcuZ2l0aHViLmlvL2FpLWxpdGVyYWN5LXN1cGVycG93ZXJzL3BsdWdpbnMvYWktbGl0ZXJhY3ktc3VwZXJwb3dlcnMvZXhwbGFuYXRpb24vaGFybmVzcy1lbmdpbmVlcmluZy8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness engineering: the missing layer</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZODpDh8WBDllSFNa1L_qNg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoYXJuZXNzLWVuZ2luZWVyaW5nLXRoZS1taXNzaW5nLWxheWVyfHxodHRwczovL2hhYml0YXQtdGhpbmtpbmcuZ2l0aHViLmlvL2FpLWxpdGVyYWN5LXN1cGVycG93ZXJzL3BsdWdpbnMvYWktbGl0ZXJhY3ktc3VwZXJwb3dlcnMvZXhwbGFuYXRpb24vaGFybmVzcy1lbmdpbmVlcmluZy8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: habitat-thinking.github.io ↗</a></p>

A term from Birgitta Boeckeler at ThoughtWorks, [harnessed here](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZODpDh8WBDllSFNa1L_qNg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxoYXJuZXNzLWVuZ2luZWVyaW5nLXRoZS1taXNzaW5nLWxheWVyfHxodHRwczovL2hhYml0YXQtdGhpbmtpbmcuZ2l0aHViLmlvL2FpLWxpdGVyYWN5LXN1cGVycG93ZXJzL3BsdWdpbnMvYWktbGl0ZXJhY3ktc3VwZXJwb3dlcnMvZXhwbGFuYXRpb24vaGFybmVzcy1lbmdpbmVlcmluZy8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) into a fuller definition. A coding harness is not a test harness; it has to verify invariants tests cannot see, like architectural decisions, naming conventions, security constraints and structural rules, because AI assistants produce plausible code that drifts quietly. The three components are context engineering (a HARNESS.md capturing stack and conventions), agent-based review, and periodic entropy checks. The framing is that the harness is not a constraint on what gets written but a continuous verification mechanism around the model.

<h2 id="cloudflare-cut-100tb-of-dns-bloat"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zw1Q7XeQqr8z1g-0XYIEXg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxjbG91ZGZsYXJlLWN1dC0xMDB0Yi1vZi1kbnMtYmxvYXR8fGh0dHBzOi8vYmxvZy5jbG91ZGZsYXJlLmNvbS9kbnMtY2FjaGUtbWVtb3J5LW9wdGltaXphdGlvbi0xMTExLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cloudflare cut 100TB of DNS bloat</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zw1Q7XeQqr8z1g-0XYIEXg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxjbG91ZGZsYXJlLWN1dC0xMDB0Yi1vZi1kbnMtYmxvYXR8fGh0dHBzOi8vYmxvZy5jbG91ZGZsYXJlLmNvbS9kbnMtY2FjaGUtbWVtb3J5LW9wdGltaXphdGlvbi0xMTExLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: blog.cloudflare.com ↗</a></p>

The [Big Pineapple](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/zw1Q7XeQqr8z1g-0XYIEXg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDgtMjgtdGVjaHxjbG91ZGZsYXJlLWN1dC0xMDB0Yi1vZi1kbnMtYmxvYXR8fGh0dHBzOi8vYmxvZy5jbG91ZGZsYXJlLmNvbS9kbnMtY2FjaGUtbWVtb3J5LW9wdGltaXphdGlvbi0xMTExLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) platform that powers 1.1.1.1, Gateway DNS, DNS Firewall and AS112 holds over 250 billion cache entries, so a single wasted byte per entry is more than 250GB across the fleet. Five successive changes to how `CacheKey` and `CacheEntry` are laid out in memory cut per-entry footprint by more than 50%, freeing roughly 100TB, which is the equivalent of 130 modern servers' RAM. Insert throughput improved 43% and lookup latency dropped 19% on top. The largest gains landed in ECS-heavy locations, where the savings compound. Cloudflare published the struct layouts, the benchmark methodology and the workload mix (56% A, 25% AAAA, 19% TXT) so anyone running a similar cache can copy the wins.
