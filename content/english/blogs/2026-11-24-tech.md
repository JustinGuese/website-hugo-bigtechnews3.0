There is no AI5 upgrade coming to cars, and the mid-cycle AI4.5 had its RAM cut from 32GB to 24GB - the refresh exists mainly because Tesla needs a memory type it can actually buy. If v15 doesn't clear the bar, there is no v16 on AI4 that will.

Two rules from the [TLDR community](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/pd1dQCS16Bv1ZtWdFRXW0A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjQtdGVjaHx0aGUtYWdlbnQtcHJvbXB0LXJ1bGVzLXRoYXQtYWN0dWFsbHktd29ya3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9jb21tdW5pdHkudGxkci50ZWNoL3Qvd2hhdC1zLXRoZS1tb3N0LXVzZWZ1bC1ydWxlLWluLXlvdXItc3lzdGVtLXByb21wdC1vci1hZ2VudHMtbWQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) are worth stealing. One: "If an action can be undone, do it and tell me what you did" - this cut routine decision turns from 64% to 21%. The pattern is reducing decision fatigue by making the agent default to action, not permission.
