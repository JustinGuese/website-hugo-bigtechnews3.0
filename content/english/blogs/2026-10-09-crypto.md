Ethereum researcher Justin Drake wants the industry to enter an operational ["bunker mode"](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/eWQ7OlNUA3aOn18kzLHjNg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDktY3J5cHRvfHx8aHR0cHM6Ly90aHJlYWRyZWFkZXJhcHAuY29tL3RocmVhZC8yMTA3ODM3MDgxMzEzNTA1NzY4Lmh0bWw_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t). The trigger is not quantum computing, but recent leaps in automated mathematics, including OpenAI's release of specialized mathematical models that have begun dismantling long-standing mathematical conjectures. Drake warns that elliptic curves carry algebraic structure that machine-discovered techniques could exploit to derive private keys from exposed public keys within weeks on large GPU clusters.

The fix is deceptively simple: move capital to clean addresses that have never broadcast an outbound transaction. Ethereum addresses are derived from the Keccak-256 hash of a secp256k1 public key. Until an address initiates a signed transaction, its raw public key remains hidden behind that preimage-resistant hash. The moment an address signs anything, its public key enters the mempool and stays permanently visible onchain. 

Drake is not calling for new cryptographic primitives or emergency hard forks today. Instead, he advises immediate address-rotation hygiene: hold balances strictly on virgin addresses, route multisig signatures offchain, and require oracles and L2 security councils to rotate one-time ECDSA keys per signature.

Vitalik Buterin took Drake's warning a step further, [outlining why](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/BlgRxKu5F5d5GB5lzJlnqQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDktY3J5cHRvfHZpdGFsaWstYmFja3MtaGFzaC1iYXNlZC1jcnlwdG9ncmFwaHktb3Zlci1sYXR0aWNlc3x8aHR0cHM6Ly90aHJlYWRyZWFkZXJhcHAuY29tL3RocmVhZC8yMTA3OTc2Mjk2MzIwMTA2ODUxLmh0bWw_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) Ethereum should avoid NIST-standardized post-quantum schemes like ML-DSA, Falcon, and general lattice-based primitives (such as Learning With Errors and FHE). Buterin argues AI models could compress 50 years of human mathematical cryptanalysis into two years. He drew parallels to how the General Number Field Sieve systematically degraded RSA's effective bit-security over decades of incremental algorithmic optimization. 

Ethereum's lean post-quantum roadmap is ditching lattices and lattice-based zero-knowledge commitments entirely. The target is hash-only signatures: Winternitz One-Time Signatures (WOTS) and SPHINCS+. Hash functions lack the rich algebraic structures that elliptic curves and lattice problems expose to algorithmic solvers. 

The rub for protocol architects is that you cannot build public-key encryption from hash functions alone. While transaction authorization can survive on hash trees, private messaging, VPN handshakes, and onchain encrypted state still require asymmetric hardness assumptions. For now, Buterin advises moving multisig coordinator confirmations offchain and passing encrypted payloads over point-to-point channels rather than publishing raw ciphertexts to public ledgers.
