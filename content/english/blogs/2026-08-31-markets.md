<h2 id="what-actually-mattered">What actually mattered</h2>

The OpenAI - Cursor cutoff is the day's cleanest second-order story, and the headline number is misleading. OpenAI models serve ~5% of Cursor traffic today, so ending access by Nov. 12 is a rounding error on OpenAI's revenue - the real question is where the remaining 95% of coding-agent demand consolidates, and GitHub's Agentic Workflows release, which runs Copilot, Claude Code, Gemini, and Codex engines side by side with per-run `max-ai-credits` budgets, makes Microsoft the structural beneficiary of every model-vendor feud regardless of who wins. That's the trade: not OpenAI, not SpaceX, but the neutral distribution layer.

The second genuinely new fact is Anthropic's Claude Code limit change. The "permanent 25% weekly limit increase" effective Sept. 14 nets out as a **17% reduction** against the temporary boost in effect today, and Anthropic deleted the original post and reissued a clarification. Combined with the Cursor cutoff, this is a demand headwind for Anthropic's coding-agent usage dressed as a gift - and a competitive opening for OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot. It does not change our view on Anthropic's overall enterprise trajectory (the alignment self-improvement report and Apple's multi-Mac-Studio event featuring Anthropic are both genuinely positive), but it is a real near-term friction point in the highest-velocity coding-agent segment.

<h2 id="agentic-payments-are-now-a-three-front-war">Agentic payments are now a three-front war</h2>

The x402 year-one numbers - 169M payments across ~590K buyers and 100K sellers, with AWS adding native CloudFront support, Cloudflare running its own facilitator, and Google building x402 into its AP2 stablecoin rail - mark the point where agent-payments infrastructure stopped being a demo and started being plumbing. Stripe securing the "Instinct" shopping agent and Modern Treasury emerging as a full-stack PSP across ACH, wires, RTP, FedNow, cards, and stablecoins are the incumbent responses; Zelle's India expansion with a stablecoin is the bank-rail response. The pressure lands on Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal - none of which we hold, and PayPal's 16% premarket drop after the Stripe/Advent pursuit collapsed at reportedly $60.50/share is the market pricing that in real time. Coinbase is the listed beneficiary of x402 adoption, and this is the strongest fundamental signal for that thesis in months.

<h2 id="the-rack-scale-bottleneck-trade-again">The rack-scale bottleneck trade, again</h2>

Musk's post that ~15GW of 2027 AI compute can't be turned on due to transformers, wiring, liquid-cooling, chillers, and networking constraints is the framing that matters for the book. Cisco's Secure AI Factory expansion to Supermicro rack-scale liquid- and air-cooled systems starting October - splitting networking between Silicon One N9300 and Spectrum-X N9100, targeting 1,000 - 100,000+ GPU clusters on Vera Rubin/Blackwell - is a direct order-flow catalyst, and the power-constraint narrative structurally favors vertically integrated players over less-integrated peers. This is the same argument we made on Friday for NVDA at conviction 4; nothing in today's material cuts against it, and Musk's post sharpens it.

<h2 id="datadog-as-the-telemetry-layer">Datadog as the telemetry layer</h2>

Two Datadog stories point the same direction. The $1M/month savings from switching Claude Code/agent defaults from Opus 4.8 to Sonnet (~60% lower token pricing) is a concrete demand signal favoring Anthropic Sonnet over Opus at agent workloads - and a live demonstration that Datadog's AI Costs feature is the tool enterprises use to find those savings. The federal renewal advisory, with OneGov pricing (OpenAI $1/agency, Anthropic $1/seat, Google $0.47/agency) expiring Sept. 30, positions Datadog as the cost-telemetry layer for a material FY27 federal-spend catalyst that flows to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft. We are moving DDOG from neutral to positive; the FinOps lead is now showing up in both commercial and federal demand signals.

<h2 id="thinning-the-rest">Thinning the rest</h2>

Slack's Slackbot Skill Sets, Deep Research, and Big Mode are real product, but this is the fourth consecutive week of Salesforce-AI headlines and the monetization delta versus Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace remains unproven - neutral stands. The DEF CON signals and the Aug. 26 METR report pointing to an AI-driven major cyberattack within six months raise enterprise demand for cloud-delivered security (Datadog, Cisco, Cloudflare) and further undermine the case for network-appliance incumbents, which supports Friday's PANW negative. The OpenAI Persistent-Sol agent-civilization reports (METR/Redwood, 38 and 91 pages) and the Codex memory-writer exfiltration issue (#41711) are a genuine safety/trust overhang for OpenAI that favors Anthropic and GitHub Copilot in enterprise evals - worth tracking, not yet a position mover. Apple's Ternus transition, the Vision Pro MLB broadcast, and the foldable roadmap are well-covered third-week narratives with no listed supplier read; the Walmart Apple Pay/Google Pay reversal is a modest distribution win for Apple and Google with no book impact. GPT-6 "Astra" evals crossing the Critical cyber capability threshold with workloads paused for safeguards is the frontier-release timing risk to watch into September.

A busy day, but the conviction is concentrated: the rack-scale bottleneck argument, the coding-agent share shift, and Datadog's telemetry positioning are where today's material actually moves a business.
