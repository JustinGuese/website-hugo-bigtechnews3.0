The attack was only worth $2 million. The real damage came from the people who saw what the attacker did and decided to join in.

An attacker used a privileged bridge-minter role to mint billions, then quintillions, of unbacked ACE on BNB Chain, sold them for roughly $2M, and crashed the price to about $0.0001 against $0.04 on exchanges. Then the free-for-all started: traders bought the worthless tokens and sent them through [Fusionist's bridge](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/IWFQbn0s938bv0ltZgnZ_Q/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjMtY3J5cHRvfHx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlYmxvY2suY28vbmV3cy9kZWZpLzIwMjYtMTEtMjItYS0yLW1pbGxpb24tZXhwbG9pdC1sZWFkcy10by1hLTMtNy1taWxsaW9uLWZyZWUtZm9yLWFsbC1hcy10cmFkZXJzLWVtcHR5LWZ1c2lvbmlzdHMtYWNlLWJyaWRnZS00NDQ4Mzg_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t), emptying its 53.7 million real ACE - about $3.7M - across 24 addresses. One wallet turned $50 into $17,670. About $1.87M of the attacker's proceeds went through Tornado Cash.

Fusionist told followers to pause ACE activity on BSC and hasn't updated since. Gate rolled back ACE/USDT trades, leaving some users with negative balances, and will delist its ACE perp. ACE is down about 20%.
