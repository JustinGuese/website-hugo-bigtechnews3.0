Junior earns more but takes losses first; senior earns less but has a built-in buffer against rate shifts. In an 80/20 Senior/Junior split on a 10% APY vault, Senior earns 8% and Junior earns 18%. A 5% drawdown leaves Senior unchanged while Junior absorbs the full loss at a 25% decline.

Looping traditionally involves up to 40 rounds of borrow/swap/deposit - hundreds of clicks - with risks of negative spreads and liquidation. Shortcuts like cheat loops require 10x capital or flash loans that charge fees on the full notional position. Mezzanine's first vault, the Hastra Optimizer, accepts USDC or wYLDS and loops Hastra's $PRIME and $AUTO tokens across lending markets backed by US home equity and car loans, with withdrawals taking up to two weeks per epoch. Tranche tokens are composable primitives built for Pendle integration or use as collateral elsewhere.

Merchants usually decline agent purchases because the agent looks like an unauthorized bot. Sardine's [Know Your Human protocol](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/CShl1kTXfYTp7mgtxYPr4g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktY3J5cHRvfGtub3cteW91ci1odW1hbi1zdG9wcGluZy1tZXJjaGFudHMtZnJvbS1kZWNsaW5pbmctYWktYWdlbnQtcHVyY2hhc2VzfHxodHRwczovL3RocmVhZHJlYWRlcmFwcC5jb20vdGhyZWFkLzIxMTkxNTc2MDIxNDgwMjQ4MTEuaHRtbD91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) walks through a test: set up an account with KYC and payment details at crypto.sardine.ai, point an agent such as Muse, Instinct, or Dots at the site and watch the purchase fail, then use the same prompt with an agent parameter so Sardine's protocol passes the human's device identifiers and IP to the merchant, and the $30 ETH purchase goes through.

The engineering problem is not payment rails - it's proving to a merchant's fraud stack that a human authorized the agent. Sardine's approach reuses the device fingerprinting and IP reputation infrastructure already built for human fraud detection, just re-labeled for agent delegation.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/AlwyTFmSjWLLwDsmdC9j4w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktY3J5cHRvfGFlcm9kcm9tZXMtbWV0YWRleDAzLWEtbmV3LW1hcmtldC1yYXRlLWZvci1saXF1aWRpdHl8fGh0dHBzOi8veC5jb20vd2FnbWlBbGV4YW5kZXIvc3RhdHVzLzIxMTg3OTY3MjI1MTYxNzMwMjU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: x.com ↗</a></p>

Traditional DEX LP compensation is capped by current swap fee revenue, a constraint that causes liquidity to drain when volume drops, prevents new markets from bootstrapping ahead of organic trading activity, and misaligns tokenholders with LPs. [Aerodrome's MetaDEX03 design](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/AlwyTFmSjWLLwDsmdC9j4w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktY3J5cHRvfGFlcm9kcm9tZXMtbWV0YWRleDAzLWEtbmV3LW1hcmtldC1yYXRlLWZvci1saXF1aWRpdHl8fGh0dHBzOi8veC5jb20vd2FnbWlBbGV4YW5kZXIvc3RhdHVzLzIxMTg3OTY3MjI1MTYxNzMwMjU_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) breaks this ceiling by paying LPs in AERO, a token that, when staked, earns a share of total network revenue including dynamic fee premiums, liquidity payments, and MEV capture - so staked AERO yields can exceed any single pool's point-in-time fee output.

The token capitalizes claims on future network productivity rather than a fixed fee split, so the protocol can subsidize current liquidity costs at a flexible multiplier as the network grows. The catch: token issuance to LPs transfers future earnings claims from existing holders, and the model holds only when the value the liquidity creates exceeds the dilution issued to attract it.
