The bottleneck to scaling enterprise AI has moved past raw model reasoning capabilities and into system context, state tracking, and permissioned data access. Traditional databases are built for human latency profiles and low-concurrency schema updates. To keep autonomous workers from hallucinating or conflicting on shared states, modern data architectures require immutable log layers, programmatic permission boundaries, and context stores that retain institutional operational state even when engineering teams swap underlying foundation models.
