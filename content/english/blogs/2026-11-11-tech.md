[Ring unveiled two new devices](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/hhThEEwFwVm1W9V-BJxvrg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtdGVjaHxyaW5ncy1kcm9uZS1jYW0tYW5kLXJvdmVyLWNhbS1wdXQtYXV0b25vbW91cy1zdXJ2ZWlsbGFuY2UtaW4tdGhlLWFpc2xlfHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5jbmJjLmNvbS8yMDI2LzExLzEwL2FtYXpvbnMtcmluZy11bnZlaWxzLWZseWluZy1kcm9uZS1yb2JvdC10by1wYXRyb2wtaG9tZXMtYnVzaW5lc3Nlcy5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) that turn the company from a passive camera vendor into an autonomous patrol company. The $250 Drone Cam is an indoor flying camera with LED lights that follows a preset path when alarms trigger, flying up to eight minutes before returning to its dock. The $1,000 Rover Cam is an autonomous robot for businesses, repurposing Amazon's Astro, with a display and an extendable periscope camera on a two-wheeled base.

The drone was first previewed in 2020 as the Always Home Cam; it's taken six years to ship. It has two plastic-encased propellers and emits a loud buzz, which is either a feature (deterrent) or a bug (annoying), depending on your threat model. The Drone Cam ships in the US on January 27 pending FCC approval; the Rover ships December 16.

For engineers, the interesting part isn't the hardware. It's that Ring - which has a documented history of police partnerships and recent backlash over AI features - now owns autonomous physical presence in your home. The attack surface isn't just a camera feed; it's a flying device with rotors that can be hijacked. The privacy calculus changed the moment the camera could move itself to where you are.

[Apple's Claris subsidiary is testing Harbor](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/RnN-CEyD_fA7NjBNEQb_Ow/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtdGVjaHxhcHBsZXMtaGFyYm9yLXR1cm5zLW1hY3MtaW50by1hLXByaXZhdGUtYWktY2x1c3Rlcnx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubWFjcnVtb3JzLmNvbS8yMDI2LzExLzEwL2FwcGxlLWNsYXJpcy1oYXJib3ItYXBwLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), an app that turns Apple silicon Macs into private AI inference servers. The headline feature: connecting multiple Macs over Thunderbolt 5 to pool unified memory, letting you run larger models with higher concurrency than any single machine could handle. The app automatically detects Thunderbolt 5-connected devices, lets you browse and load open-weight models, and requires no manual configuration.

Harbor is in early access with a removed App Store listing, and its sign-up page asks for a company name - suggesting a business focus. It's a direct competitor to tools like Exo and LM Studio, but with a key difference: Apple's software stack, Apple's hardware, and presumably Apple's polish.

The engineering implication is straightforward. If you're running local models today, you're manually sharding across machines or renting cloud GPUs. Harbor promises to make a Thunderbolt 5 cluster of Mac Studios a viable private inference tier - no cloud, no data leaving the building, no per-token billing. The question is whether Apple prices it as a developer tool or as an enterprise product.

[A new analysis from AI Futures](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/8pCD6ktFPb7bQCLw4SuP8w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtdGVjaHxhaS1mdXR1cmVzLXNheXMtdGhlLWludGVsbGlnZW5jZS1leHBsb3Npb24tYXJyaXZlcy1pbi0yMDI3fHxodHRwczovL2Jsb2cuYWlmdXR1cmVzLm9yZy9wL3EzNS10aW1lbGluZXMtdXBkYXRlLWxvb2tzLWxpa2UtdGhlP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) argues AI progress is accelerating faster than expected, citing a METR report estimating Anthropic is making progress about twice as fast as historical rates, with ~80% of the acceleration due to AI. They now forecast Superhuman AI Researcher (SAR) arriving in 2027, with a 35-65% chance of Top-Expert-Dominating AI (TED-AI) before 2028.

The authors urge an industry-wide pause or pace on frontier AI development, warning of risks from misaligned SAR and potential theft of training recipes by China. Whether you buy the timeline or not, the underlying claim - that AI is accelerating AI development - is measurable and hard to dismiss. For engineers, the practical implication is that capabilities you're planning for in 2029 might arrive in 2027.

[Anthropic plans to sell 825 million shares at $70 to $78 a share](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/JTpoQDMb2gdAfLhmymG6ig/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTEtdGVjaHxhbnRocm9waWMtZmlsZXMtZm9yLWEtMTMtdHJpbGxpb24taXBvfHxodHRwczovL3d3dy53c2ouY29tL2ZpbmFuY2Uvc3RvY2tzL2FudGhyb3BpYy1pcG8tdmFsdWF0aW9uLTgzMzdiNjY5P3N0PVRUS2t3biZyZWZsaW5rPWRlc2t0b3B3ZWJzaGFyZV9wZXJtYWxpbmsmdXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) in its IPO, expected to start trading Thursday next week.
