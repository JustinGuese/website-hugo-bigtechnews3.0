Ethereum core developers have paused setting a firm release date for the upcoming Glamsterdam hard fork. Audit firm ChainSecurity disclosed that [EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 introduce serious vulnerabilities](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DrOkzaA7Ufa7yUpyj_7a0w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjctY3J5cHRvfGdsYW1zdGVyZGFtLWdhcy1saW1pdHMtc3RhbGwtb24tZWlwLTgwMzctcmVmdW5kLWJ1Z3N8fGh0dHBzOi8veC5jb20vZGVjZW50cmVrL3N0YXR1cy8yMTE0NDA1MTQ5NzI5MDc5NjA3P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) affecting at least 13 live DeFi protocols. Glamsterdam aims to raise the block gas limit from 60M to 200M while containing state growth, but EIP-8037 alters core EVM execution guarantees.

EIP-8037 modifies gas mechanics by refilling gas when storage slots are freed, breaking the 63/64 rule assumptions that DeFi error handlers rely on to catch nested execution failures. Under EIP-150, an outer call retains 1/64th of the parent call’s gas, guaranteeing that an outer contract can catch an out-of-gas error in a child call and run fallback or recovery logic. By introducing dynamic refills mid-execution, child calls can alter the remaining gas budget unexpectedly, preventing outer error handlers from completing and leaving contracts in broken states. 

Following the disclosure, teams from the Ethereum Foundation and Ethlabs deployed automated scanning agents to evaluate the mainnet contract registry. The agents used a collaborative GitHub coordination structure - claiming targets through issue comments, running symbolic analysis, and storing structured findings without human triage. The analysis paused Glamsterdam's rollout and is now running against EIP-8198, which proposes reducing consensus slot times from 12s to 10s ahead of the Hegotá upgrade.

The token resolves a structural limitation in corporate stablecoins: standard collateralized stablecoins (like USDC or USDT) cannot pay native yield to US retail holders without being classified as securities, leaving an estimated $64 billion in stablecoin assets idle. SOFIE bridges this gap by letting users convert their interest-bearing token 1:1 into SoFiUSD. SoFiUSD functions as a pure transactional stablecoin running on Ethereum and Solana, routing directly into Mastercard’s settlement network to clear card transactions. For protocol engineers, this design provides a blueprint for interfacing regulated banking balance sheets directly with public L1 liquidity pools.

Rather than minting or redeeming USDC entirely against the issuer's central treasury reserves, Match pools mint and redeem requests into hourly auctions.

The auction pairs institutional entities looking to acquire native USDC with market makers or desks looking to offload it for fiat wire settlement. When orders match within the system, redeeming participants exit at their specified limit pricing while minting participants receive a programmatic rebate. The base token contract, mint allowances, and collateral reserve requirements remain untouched; Circle is simply internalizing retail-versus-institutional order flow to lower wire and settlement overhead.

Analyzing Bitcoin daily price closes between 2018 and 2026, the data shows that a standard 10x perp long suffered a 47% liquidation rate over a 180-day holding period - even on trades where the asset price ultimately finished higher than the entry point.

Perp liquidations trigger on intra-candle wick volatility, creating severe path dependency for long-horizon position sizing. Options eliminate intra-period liquidation entirely, requiring settlement only at expiration. The research notes that onchain options volume (currently ~$5B notional monthly, dominated by Derive with over 90% of onchain premiums) will scale through symbiotic integration rather than competition: options makers hedge net-delta exposure using perpetual swaps.
