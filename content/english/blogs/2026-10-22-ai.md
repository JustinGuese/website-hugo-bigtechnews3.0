<h2 id="ant-groups-humming-modular-execution-kernels-for-quantized-inference"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/VyO4F_A4SehwDz1iNB3Nlg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjItYWl8YW50LWdyb3Vwcy1odW1taW5nLW1vZHVsYXItZXhlY3V0aW9uLWtlcm5lbHMtZm9yLXF1YW50aXplZC1pbmZlcmVuY2V8fGh0dHBzOi8vdmxsbS5haS9ibG9nLzIwMjYtMTAtMjEtaHVtbWluZz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ant Group&#x27;s Humming: Modular execution kernels for quantized inference</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/VyO4F_A4SehwDz1iNB3Nlg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjItYWl8YW50LWdyb3Vwcy1odW1taW5nLW1vZHVsYXItZXhlY3V0aW9uLWtlcm5lbHMtZm9yLXF1YW50aXplZC1pbmZlcmVuY2V8fGh0dHBzOi8vdmxsbS5haS9ibG9nLzIwMjYtMTAtMjEtaHVtbWluZz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: vllm.ai ↗</a></p>

Ant Group and the vLLM project open-sourced [Humming](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/VyO4F_A4SehwDz1iNB3Nlg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjItYWl8YW50LWdyb3Vwcy1odW1taW5nLW1vZHVsYXItZXhlY3V0aW9uLWtlcm5lbHMtZm9yLXF1YW50aXplZC1pbmZlcmVuY2V8fGh0dHBzOi8vdmxsbS5haS9ibG9nLzIwMjYtMTAtMjEtaHVtbWluZz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), a low-level compute library built to optimize inference across heterogeneous quantization formats. Humming works on modern Nvidia GPUs (Turing through newer families) as well as T-Head's Zhenwu 810E silicon, unifying kernels across WNA16, W8A8, W4A8, and W4A4 layouts for both dense models and MoE layers.

Current inference frameworks often bind specific quantization quantization arithmetic directly to the target hardware matrix engines, complicating kernel customization. Humming breaks this layer down, separating quantization decompression routines from the underlying GEMM scheduling. In production testing against Marlin, FlashInfer, and DeepGEMM, the engine consistently cut latency and memory footprints when hosting ultra-wide networks like DeepSeek-V3.
