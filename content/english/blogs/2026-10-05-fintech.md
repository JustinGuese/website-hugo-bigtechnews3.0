Card network consortium EMVCo published a draft standard, [Agentic Payments - Framework for Specifications](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DSh3VXLDbQp3SgDTaL4JGA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtZmludGVjaHxlbXZjby1kcmFmdHMtaW50ZW50LXNwZWNpZmljYXRpb25zLWZvci1tYWNoaW5lLW9yaWdpbmF0ZWQtcGF5bWVudHN8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LnB5bW50cy5jb20vbmV3cy9hcnRpZmljaWFsLWludGVsbGlnZW5jZS8yMDI2L2VtdmNvLWZyYW1ld29yay10YXJnZXRzLWNvbnN1bWVyLWludGVudC1hZ2VudGljLXBheW1lbnRzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), designed to standardize how card networks process payments initiated by autonomous software. The proposed framework introduces "Intent Services," which append cryptographically verifiable, consumer-authorized boundaries to the payment payload.

Under the specification, when an agent acts on behalf of a user, it carries structured parameters - such as maximum item price, explicit vendor categories, and recurring transaction policies - through the authorization pipeline. Rather than forcing card networks or issuing processors to guess whether a browser automation script or API request is legitimate, the standard creates a verifiable audit trail that separates consumer consent from the agent's identity. Risk engines at issuers and gateways will need updates to ingest these intent tokens to evaluate chargeback disputes and synthetic fraud.

The era of cheap venture subsidies for financial apps has ended, leaving market consolidation in its wake. In an essay titled [*Fintech is Dead*](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/oWiTmMg_GFrbKh0WaM2xIA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtZmludGVjaHxjYXBpdGFsLXByZXNzdXJlcy1yZXNoYXBlLXByaXZhdGUtbWFya2V0c3x8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubGlua2VkaW4uY29tL3B1bHNlL2ZpbnRlY2gtZGVhZC1zaW1vbi10YXlsb3ItLTFtb25lLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), Simon Taylor notes that APIs, digital cards, and cloud hosting have become commoditized utilities, with global fintech venture share dropping from 14.5% in 2021 to 12.3% in 2025. Across European hubs, [London's fintech scene](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/MMbrigMl7dXXPMxl4iwjZg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtZmludGVjaHxjYXBpdGFsLXByZXNzdXJlcy1yZXNoYXBlLXByaXZhdGUtbWFya2V0c3x8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuamFwYW50aW1lcy5jby5qcC9idXNpbmVzcy8yMDI2LzA5LzMwL3RlY2gvbG9uZG9uLWZpbnRlY2gtc2hha2VvdXQvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is seeing funding sink to its lowest levels since 2016, pushing mid-tier consumer apps into distress sales while surviving capital shifts into B2B platforms and scaled operators like Revolut and Monzo.

Meanwhile, institutional data pipes are consolidating: [Bloomberg acquired Canoe Intelligence](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/4gUnK_5ZWm-KLrjeR9QipA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtZmludGVjaHxjYXBpdGFsLXByZXNzdXJlcy1yZXNoYXBlLXByaXZhdGUtbWFya2V0c3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9saW5rcy50bGRybmV3c2xldHRlci5jb20vaEtERWRaP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) to link its unstructured PDF ingestion pipelines directly into Bloomberg PORT Enterprise. In retail checkout, [PayPal warned](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/tRfDMlqlf-sYoQmGKzbYGg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMDUtZmludGVjaHxjYXBpdGFsLXByZXNzdXJlcy1yZXNoYXBlLXByaXZhdGUtbWFya2V0c3x8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cucGF5bWVudHNkaXZlLmNvbS9uZXdzL3BheXBhbC1oaW50cy1hdC1ibnBsLWhvbGlkYXktdXNlLzgzMjAxMC8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) that 75% of consumers who use installment options plan to lean on buy-now-pay-later splits for holiday shopping amid mounting financial strain.
