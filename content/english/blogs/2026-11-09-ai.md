The paper embeds AI-driven automation of AI research into a standard economic growth model, governed by three forces: diminishing returns, bottlenecks (complementarity), and spillovers from AI to the rest of the economy.

The model gives a framework for when RSI could lead to an "economic singularity" - and when it can't. If diminishing returns dominate, progress stalls. If bottlenecks dominate, progress is paced by the slowest component. If spillovers dominate, you get explosive growth. Understanding which force actually governs is critical for anyone making long-term plans about infrastructure spend or model procurement.

Anthropic's [Project EVE](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/2siyBdAQcn1Ahd5TwI-YGQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktYWl8cHJvamVjdC1ldmUtY2xhdWRlLXBsYXlzLW1tb3JwZ3N8fGh0dHBzOi8vd3d3LmFudGhyb3BpYy5jb20vcmVzZWFyY2gvcHJvamVjdC1ldmUtY2xhdWRlLXBsYXlzLW1tb3JwZ3M_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) had Claude play two MMORPGs in the EVE Universe: EVE Online and EVE Frontier. In EVE Online, Claude played as a lone trader in a simulated market, with newer models making consistent profits where simple algorithms lost money. Average earnings tripled from Claude Opus 4.6 to Claude Fable 5.

In EVE Frontier, teams of four Claude agents worked through long missions in the live game, requiring the most capable model to write code to handle aspects of the game. This is a low-stakes testbed for long-horizon tasks involving coordination, resource management, and economic decision-making - exactly the kind of work agents will be asked to do in production systems.

<h2 id="can-llms-redesign-themselves-as-they-train"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/C2shZwZsVjA05XicXtJzTA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktYWl8Y2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWlufHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5kb3VibGVhaS5jb20vcmVzZWFyY2gvY2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWluP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Can LLMs Redesign Themselves as They Train?</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/C2shZwZsVjA05XicXtJzTA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktYWl8Y2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWlufHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5kb3VibGVhaS5jb20vcmVzZWFyY2gvY2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWluP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: doubleai.com ↗</a></p>

A [white paper from DoubleAI](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/C2shZwZsVjA05XicXtJzTA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMDktYWl8Y2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWlufHxodHRwczovL3d3dy5kb3VibGVhaS5jb20vcmVzZWFyY2gvY2FuLWxsbXMtcmVkZXNpZ24tdGhlbXNlbHZlcy1hcy10aGV5LXRyYWluP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) describes putting an AI agent inside a neural network's training run to redesign the network on the fly via "mechanistic intervention." The agent reads the network's internal state and changes the data, objective, update rule, or architecture - building its own toolkit like the "phantom gradient."

The results are striking. On CLRS-Text, code layers grown during training raised accuracy on in-between input sizes from 33% to 79%. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash acting as an agent on itself raised its AIME 2026 and HMMT 2026 accuracy from 64% to 71%. This demonstrates a path toward models that can train and re-architect themselves - moving beyond the current paradigm where a transformer transforms everything but itself.
