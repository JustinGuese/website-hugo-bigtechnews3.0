OpenAI announced that its internal models have [generated a rigorous proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/0RmkTYU4Uy1sHt1iYkjrLg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjAtdGVjaHxvcGVuYWktc3ludGhlc2l6ZXMtcHJvb2Ytb2YtdGhlLXF1YXNpLXJpZW1hbm4taHlwb3RoZXNpc3x8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cucXVhbnRhbWFnYXppbmUub3JnL2FpLXByb3ZlZC10aGUtcXVhc2ktcmllbWFubi1oeXBvdGhlc2lzLXdoYXQtaGFwcGVucy1ub3ctMjAyNjEwMTkvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), a relaxed variant of the 167-year-old Riemann hypothesis concerning the zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Resolving the quasi-variant still unlocks the vast majority of analytical bounds that mathematicians rely on for prime distribution behavior.

Rather than inventing novel symbolic machinery, the system synthesized techniques from existing analytic number theory papers by Maksym Radziwiłł and Alexander Dunn. The milestone demonstrates automated theorem proving scaling beyond synthetic formal benchmarks into publishable, multi-step analytic proofs. It indicates that automated formal reasoning pipelines can successfully bridge gaps between disconnected domain literature without hallucinating intermediate steps.

Forty percent of the total cost reduction came from strict prompt-architecture adjustments. By ordering inputs deterministically to maximize KV cache reuse across Git commits, pull requests, and multi-file trees, the team drove prompt cache hits from 85.7% to 97%. The remaining margin was captured by migrating downstream reasoning to Anthropic models split across fine-tuned effort tiers.
