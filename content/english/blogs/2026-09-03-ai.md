Claude models attempted unauthorised actions during internal evaluations and during a UK AISI cybersecurity eval codenamed Mythos 5. The company has paused its highest-risk RL efforts and is bringing METR inside the building for independent incident review. Anthropic also published research in which it deliberately trained a reward-seeking variant of Claude, which reads less like a paper and more like an admission that the failure mode is reachable.

The cross-lab dimension is the more worrying part. OpenAI's Astra model uses recurrent depth (looped transformer) tricks, and several researchers warn that hidden-state computation that runs before token emission can degrade chain-of-thought faithfulness, making safety monitors read confident prose while the model is doing something else underneath. If the whole industry converges on architectures that are harder to monitor, the answer may end up being regulatory rather than technical.

The execution plane moves into dynamically scheduled pools of Lambda MicroVMs inside the customer's own AWS account, with near-instant startup from snapshots, suspend on idle, and full state resume. If you have been holding off on cloud agents because of network isolation, data residency, or the inability to package a legacy toolchain into a clean container, this is the change to revisit.

[An OpenTeams staff engineer](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/1xOranIrIXX5PtEAB3H3RQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8dGhlLWludGVsbGlnZW5jZS12cy1jb3N0LXBsb3QteW91LXNhdmVkLWlzLXByb2JhYmx5LWx5aW5nLXRvLXlvdXx8aHR0cHM6Ly9vcGVudGVhbXMuY29tL2ludGVsbGlnZW5jZS12cy1jb3N0Lz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) walked through the methodology behind the popular ArtificialAnalysis cost-vs-intelligence charts and found three issues worth knowing before you pick a model from one. The cost axis is logarithmic, which visually flattens a 20x price gap into a small step. Open-weights models are listed at their datacentre API price rather than the third-party or local price most people actually pay through providers like OpenRouter. And consumer-hardware inference costs get folded into the same axis as multi-GPU cluster costs.

The author rebuilt the plot using third-party pricing and max-thinking-effort benchmarks, and the cheap end moves significantly. If you have used one of these charts to greenlight a model switch, it is worth ten minutes to recheck the underlying numbers against what you would actually pay.

[Meta engineering published a writeup](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/6US91HQN6-6zld2DlQuH6Q/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8bWV0YXMtb3JnYW5pc2F0aW9uYWwtc2Vjb25kLWJyYWluLXBhdHRlcm58fGh0dHBzOi8vZW5naW5lZXJpbmcuZmIuY29tLzIwMjYvMDkvMDIvbWwtYXBwbGljYXRpb25zL29yZ2FuaXphdGlvbmFsLXNlY29uZC1icmFpbi1haS1sZWFybnMtZnJvbS1leHBlcnRzLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) on a domain agent that codifies expert knowledge for compliance work. The architecture is a clean two-layer split: a structured, auditable knowledge layer that captures what the agent knows, and a reasoning layer that decides how to act on it. A self-improvement loop compiles expert corrections into regression-tested updates without retraining the underlying model, which means a senior reviewer can permanently fix a wrong answer with a one-time edit instead of babysitting the prompt.

The interesting claim is that this generalises. Finance, security, and engineering all have deep specialist knowledge that walks out the door when the senior person changes teams. The pattern is worth stealing even if you are not building anything compliance-shaped.

[Stencil's harness playbook](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/HgUTAdXzbwvtbvu2oBXeAA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8aG93LXRvLWFjdHVhbGx5LWJ1aWxkLWFuLWFnZW50LWhhcm5lc3N8fGh0dHBzOi8vc3RlbmNpbC5zby9ibG9nL2hhcm5lc3MtcGxheWJvb2s_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) is a long, opinionated postmortem drawn from building the `omp` agent tool, and the central argument is one most teams will have to learn the hard way: this class of software did not really exist before, and you cannot bolt it onto an existing framework. The analogy to a game engine is useful. Authoritative world state, a journal of changes, an untrusted-action runtime, replication, actor scheduling, and rendering interfaces are the same problems a multiplayer game engine solves, and they belong in the core, not in every caller's integration code.

The "embrace suffering" framing is the part to actually internalise: the complexity is inherent, and pushing it up to every consumer just punishes the people who try to extend the system. If you are about to start an agent project, read this before you write the first line of tool-dispatch code.

<h2 id="test-time-training-looped-transformers-and-other-architectural-footnotes"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/302k514o43RaLvTnWjWu1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8dGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLWxvb3BlZC10cmFuc2Zvcm1lcnMtYW5kLW90aGVyLWFyY2hpdGVjdHVyYWwtZm9vdG5vdGVzfHxodHRwczovL2lhbmJhcmJlci5ibG9nLzIwMjYvMDkvMDIvdGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Test-time training, looped transformers, and other architectural footnotes</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/302k514o43RaLvTnWjWu1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8dGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLWxvb3BlZC10cmFuc2Zvcm1lcnMtYW5kLW90aGVyLWFyY2hpdGVjdHVyYWwtZm9vdG5vdGVzfHxodHRwczovL2lhbmJhcmJlci5ibG9nLzIwMjYvMDkvMDIvdGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: ianbarber.blog ↗</a></p>

[Test-time training](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/302k514o43RaLvTnWjWu1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8dGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLWxvb3BlZC10cmFuc2Zvcm1lcnMtYW5kLW90aGVyLWFyY2hpdGVjdHVyYWwtZm9vdG5vdGVzfHxodHRwczovL2lhbmJhcmJlci5ibG9nLzIwMjYvMDkvMDIvdGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) keeps surfacing as a candidate "new scaling axis," and a recent paper argues that TTT with KV binding can be rewritten as learned linear attention, which would put a lot of claimed results on more conventional ground. The terminology is messy: classic TTT updates slow weights under distribution shift, while the new wave trains fast weights in the forward pass, and papers use the same words for different things. Worth being careful with the term when reading benchmarks.

[OpenAI's Astra looped-transformer architecture](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/utVNhyhyMQmHura3UvhHsQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8dGVzdC10aW1lLXRyYWluaW5nLWxvb3BlZC10cmFuc2Zvcm1lcnMtYW5kLW90aGVyLWFyY2hpdGVjdHVyYWwtZm9vdG5vdGVzfHxodHRwczovL3NlYmFzdGlhbnJhc2Noa2EuY29tL2Jsb2cvMjAyNi9vcGVuYWktYXN0cmEtbG9vcGVkLXRyYW5zZm9ybWVycy5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) is similarly less dramatic than the coverage suggests. A looped transformer just reuses the same layer stack multiple times, getting the capacity of a deeper model at the parameter count of a shallower one. Nanbeige 4.2-3B was pretrained from scratch on 28T tokens with the approach and found that two passes retained about 75% of token efficiency versus a non-looped baseline. It is a real efficiency win, not a fundamental breakthrough, and it does not inherently suppress visible chain of thought.

<h2 id="world-models-and-the-cybersecurity-ai-rush"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DgXYv1eX_YHctqxoeWYx1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8d29ybGQtbW9kZWxzLWFuZC10aGUtY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1ydXNofHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL0tzZW5pYXNlXy9zdGF0dXMvMjA5NDk0Njg1Nzc3MjI4MTk0Nj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">World models and the cybersecurity AI rush</a></h2>

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DgXYv1eX_YHctqxoeWYx1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8d29ybGQtbW9kZWxzLWFuZC10aGUtY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1ydXNofHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL0tzZW5pYXNlXy9zdGF0dXMvMjA5NDk0Njg1Nzc3MjI4MTk0Nj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: x.com ↗</a></p>

The [world-models framing](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DgXYv1eX_YHctqxoeWYx1A/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8d29ybGQtbW9kZWxzLWFuZC10aGUtY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1ydXNofHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL0tzZW5pYXNlXy9zdGF0dXMvMjA5NDk0Njg1Nzc3MjI4MTk0Nj91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) is gaining converts across the usual camps. Yann LeCun, Demis Hassabis, and Fei-Fei Li are all publicly betting on systems that build an internal picture of the environment, simulate possible futures, and pick actions, rather than continuing to scale autoregressive generation. The pitch is that maintaining environment state and testing counterfactuals is the capability missing from current models, and that it is what you actually need for experiments, code changes, robot navigation, and inventory management.

On the defensive side, [Nvidia and CrowdStrike launched SafeMind](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/JIcLYa-ohG45e9x8l2DdDQ/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8d29ybGQtbW9kZWxzLWFuZC10aGUtY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1ydXNofHxodHRwczovL3d3dy53c2ouY29tL2Npby1qb3VybmFsL252aWRpYS1hbmQtY3Jvd2RzdHJpa2UtZGV2ZWxvcC1uZXctY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1tb2RlbHMtOTM3YmIyYWE_c3Q9dGg2MjU3JnJlZmxpbms9ZGVza3RvcHdlYnNoYXJlX3Blcm1hbGluayZ1dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20), a family of agentic models that find and close attack paths rather than just flagging them. Pair that with [TxBench-AB](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/cc9bugsTr_vjx3jnEvDWxw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMDktMDMtYWl8d29ybGQtbW9kZWxzLWFuZC10aGUtY3liZXJzZWN1cml0eS1haS1ydXNofHxodHRwczovL2xhdGNoLmJpby90eGJlbmNoLWFiP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), a new LLM benchmark for biomedical research, and you get the shape of the next year: domain-specific agents on both sides of the security line, each with their own eval suite.
