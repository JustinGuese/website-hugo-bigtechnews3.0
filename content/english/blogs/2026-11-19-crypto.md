For anyone running trading systems, the notable shift is the interface: natural language becomes the order entry system. Traceability of those decisions becomes the operational problem.

[Production tokenized deposits are "on-us"](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/iiJzuibWkfJTgliE7mwNrg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTktY3J5cHRvfGhvdy10b2tlbml6ZWQtZGVwb3NpdHMtYWN0dWFsbHktc2V0dGxlfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL2JvcmphbmVpcmFfL3N0YXR1cy8yMTIyNzMxNTI4MzMzNzIxNzgzP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ), meaning they settle within a single bank's balance sheet, as seen with Citi Token Services and JPMorgan's Kinexys. Moving them between banks requires interbank settlement, since the receiving bank otherwise holds an unsecured claim on the sender.

The piece maps four balance sheet boundaries - intrabranch, interbranch, inter-affiliate, interbank - to explain why cross-institutional settlement is structurally complex. Swift's Shared Ledger locks the deposit and lets the receiving bank credit immediately, but settlement is deferred through RTGS, leaving credit exposure in between. BIS's Project Agorá takes a different route, settling via central bank reserves.

The practical takeaway: issuing a tokenized deposit on an internal ledger is necessary but not sufficient for better interbank settlement. The hard problem is credit exposure between legal entities, and no tokenization scheme removes that. Engineers building on tokenized deposits should understand which boundary they're operating at, because the settlement mechanics differ entirely.

[Coinbase Ventures mapped the agentic trading stack](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/gmgHloF2dBKZaR1h1N-9Cw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTktY3J5cHRvfHRoZS1hZ2VudGljLXRyYWRpbmctc3RhY2staGFzLW5vLW1vYXQtYXQtdGhlLWFnZW50LWxheWVyfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL2NidmVudHVyZXMvc3RhdHVzLzIxMjI3NDA1NDQ5OTY4NTIwNDQ_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) into four layers: Intelligence (data and market reasoning models), Agency (harnesses, terminals, and copilots), Execution (venue connectivity), and Compliance and Observability. In the Agency layer, OpenClaw accumulated ~250K GitHub stars in 60 days, with trading-specialized harnesses like Senpi and Supertake competing across asset classes.

The key insight: the agent itself carries no moat. Open-source models on open-source harnesses commoditize agent capability. Value sits with whoever owns the decision loop (Intent-as-a-Service), platforms with the deepest liquidity, and custodians trusted across the broadest asset class range. Coinbase Ventures is investing across harnesses and terminals, proprietary intelligence, and compliance infrastructure covering agent identity and tamper-proof decision logs.

For anyone building in this space, the compliance layer is the neglected opportunity. Traceable, accountable agent actions in financial markets aren't optional - regulators will demand them. Agent identity and tamper-proof decision logs are the infrastructure that makes autonomous trading defensible.
