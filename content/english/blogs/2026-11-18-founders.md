Software stocks lost an estimated $2 trillion early this year on the theory that AI would eat business apps. Stripe's new weekly index of the companies that bill through it says the obituary was written too soon: non-AI B2B revenue is up more than 30% year over year, about 4% above its pre-sell-off trend. That's not a rounding error, and it's not the enterprise names that cratered.

The index covers roughly 72,000 non-AI B2B businesses paying by card, mostly self-serve and product-led. Those are the long tail, not the seat-based enterprise vendors whose net revenue retention everyone watches. If you want to know whether the SaaSpocalypse actually killed demand, this is the earliest signal you'll get - and it says the long tail never stopped growing.

> A company that shuts down also drops out of the index instead of counting as a loss, so the 30% figure is a floor, not a ceiling.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/aztSBsatw2YX_QFWk_fVzw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8ZWFydGhseXMtcG9zdC1tb3J0ZW0tZGV2ZWxvcGVycy1hZG9wdGVkLWJ1c2luZXNzLWRpZG50LWZvbGxvd3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9lYXJ0aGx5LmRldi9ibG9nL2RldmVsb3BlcnMtYWRvcHRlZC1lYXJ0aGx5LWJ1c2luZXNzLWRpZG50LWZvbGxvdz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: earthly.dev ↗</a></p>

[Earthly's commercial failure](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/aztSBsatw2YX_QFWk_fVzw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8ZWFydGhseXMtcG9zdC1tb3J0ZW0tZGV2ZWxvcGVycy1hZG9wdGVkLWJ1c2luZXNzLWRpZG50LWZvbGxvd3x8aHR0cHM6Ly9lYXJ0aGx5LmRldi9ibG9nL2RldmVsb3BlcnMtYWRvcHRlZC1lYXJ0aGx5LWJ1c2luZXNzLWRpZG50LWZvbGxvdz91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) is the canonical dev-tools cautionary tale. Developers adopted the free Earthfiles build tool, but few bought Satellites, the managed build runners - because teams could run persistent build environments themselves and get similar speed. Broader adoption stalled because migration work was hard to prioritize, and interviews revealed a company-wide problem where even simple fixes sat unmerged for quarters.

The pivot to Lunar, an engineering guardrails product, addresses a problem engineering leaders already own with a budget they control, rather than relying on organic adoption. The lesson for anyone building dev infrastructure: check whether your free version already delivers the value you plan to charge for.

<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/IkpRrkQLaWTePSD_Nbp3rw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLWRlYXRoLW9mLXRoZS1iYWNrbG9nfHxodHRwczovL2ZhaW5nZXppY2h0LmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlcy8yMDI2LzExLzE3L3RoZS1kZWF0aC1vZi10aGUtYmFja2xvZy8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: faingezicht.com ↗</a></p>

[The traditional product backlog](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/IkpRrkQLaWTePSD_Nbp3rw/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTgtZm91bmRlcnN8dGhlLWRlYXRoLW9mLXRoZS1iYWNrbG9nfHxodHRwczovL2ZhaW5nZXppY2h0LmNvbS9hcnRpY2xlcy8yMDI2LzExLzE3L3RoZS1kZWF0aC1vZi10aGUtYmFja2xvZy8_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) is dying, argues this essay from experience at Apple (Radar), Vouch (Jira), and AngelList (no formal system, work picked from Slack). At AngelList, engineers resisted backlogs as "where tickets go to die," but the author defends that as a feature - filtering requests is part of an engineering manager's job to remove bottlenecks.

The sharper point: small software changes can cost about as much to try with an agent as to describe in a ticket. When that's true, the backlog stops being a planning tool and becomes a delay mechanism. How a company decides what to build reflects its culture, and the backlog's role is shifting as planning cadences shorten.
