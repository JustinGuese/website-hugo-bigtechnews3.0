The number that mattered today was Microsoft's: [more than $2 billion annually to Anthropic](#) to run Claude models inside Copilot. Whatever else happened, this is the first time the model-vendor tax on a flagship enterprise AI product has been priced in public at this scale. It cuts two ways - Copilot gets a genuinely competitive model, but Microsoft's AI gross margin story now has a named, recurring, billion-scale line item against it. We've been negative on MSFT for a week on exactly this tension; the commitment confirms it rather than changing it. The second-order read: this is a win for Anthropic's economics and a data point for every other enterprise buyer negotiating model contracts - and it puts pressure on OpenAI to lock in equivalent distribution, which is probably the context for Cloudflare's [new OpenAI partnership](#) on AI discovery.

> The agent stack is stratifying in real time: Microsoft pays for the brains, ServiceNow sells the audit trail, and Meta wants to own the wallet the agent pays with.

<h2 id="the-agent-control-plane">The agent control plane</h2>

ServiceNow shipped two things that matter more than their press-release weight suggests. [AI Control Tower](#) does governance and cost tracking for AI assets, and [AI Passport](#) issues digitally signed credentials for enterprise agents, with Nvidia as launch partner. The logic is simple: once agents act autonomously, someone has to answer "which agent did what, on whose authority, at what cost." That is a ServiceNow-shaped problem, and selling the audit trail is better margin than selling the workflow. We flagged the Nvidia non-human-identity work two weeks ago; this is that thread building, not a rehash. The non-obvious beneficiary is the identity vendor that ends up holding agent credentials - the convergence pattern we noted at Okta and peers eight days apart is now being validated from the ITSM side.

Meanwhile Meta entered the "wallet wars" with [tokenized payment credentials for AI agents](#). This is the third leg of the Meta enterprise push we've tracked - excess datacenter capacity, then enterprise AI, now payments rails. If agents transact, someone holds the credential and takes a cut, and Meta is betting it can be the wallet layer the way it was the identity layer for consumers.

<h2 id="the-plumbing">The plumbing</h2>

- [Cloudflare partnered with OpenAI](#) to improve AI discovery for its customers. After Birthday Week's AI Gateway additions and the schema-driven tooling push, this converts a feature franchise into a named relationship with the largest AI vendor. Consistent with our positive NET stance.
- [Akamai reports AI and SaaS workloads driving cloud adoption](#) - directionally right, but it's a vendor claim, and the Anthropic deal from earlier this month already did the heavy lifting for the Akamai thesis.
- [Datadog published a service-criticality guide](#) and [Amazon added a LiveTaskCount metric to ECS](#) and an [autonomous "AI NOC Engineer" reference build](#). All real, all minor - observability vendors are racing to meter agent workloads, which is the same governance theme ServiceNow is monetizing at a higher layer.
- [Replit switched its default agent provider from Anthropic to OpenAI](#). One customer switching is noise; a pattern of switching would be a pricing-power signal for the model vendors. Watch for the next data point.

<h2 id="the-thin-end-of-the-day">The thin end of the day</h2>

[Qualcomm's CEO says AI labs aim to run 100-billion-parameter models on phones by 2028](#) - a two-year-out ambition, not a roadmap with silicon attached. Apple previewed a robotic HomeView and got a retrospective on its seven-year-old gaming initiative; both are optionality narratives with no revenue model attached, and Apple stays a neutral hold in the book. Coinbase's [60x-faster advanced matching engine](#) is the most concrete engineering item of the day - unglamorous infrastructure that compounds into cost-per-trade advantage. Honeywell's Forge Cognition agent platform for industrial alarms and maintenance is a reminder that agent demand is showing up outside the developer-tools echo chamber.

The day was thin below the Microsoft line. The theme that's genuinely building is agent governance and payments - ServiceNow, Cloudflare, Meta all moved it forward this week. The theme that's getting stale is model-availability headlines; the money is now in who pays for them.
