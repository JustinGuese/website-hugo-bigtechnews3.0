Rackspace is pulling the plug on its Spot Kubernetes platform, and the deadline is brutal: December 8 at 5 PM Central. A November 17 breach let attackers use compromised credentials to reach customer clusters, read Secrets, and list nodes and volumes. If you run workloads on Spot, you have less than two weeks to migrate everything and download your data - after that, remaining workloads get terminated and stored data becomes inaccessible.

The breach notice has a specific threat to hunt for: Rackspace tells all Spot customers to treat API tokens, refresh tokens, kubeconfigs, and Kubernetes Secrets as compromised, and to review logs for suspicious activity from IP 23.224.79.106 and range 23.224.0.0/15, plus pod prefixes like "i1", "glm-", and "probe". Even customers who didn't get an earlier breach notification are told to assume exposure. Rackspace says it found no evidence the incident spread to its core infrastructure or other customer environments.

> Every Spot API token, refresh token, kubeconfig, and Kubernetes Secret should be treated as compromised, even if you never got a breach notification.
