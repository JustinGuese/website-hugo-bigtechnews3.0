<p class="small mb-4"><a href="https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZwWTV8x7hQcaumeeW9hA0w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctYWl8Yml0d2lzZS16ZXJvLXJsLW9uLXRwdXMta2lsbGluZy10aGUtb2ZmLXBvbGljeS1nYXB8fGh0dHBzOi8vZGV2ZWxvcGVycy5nb29nbGVibG9nLmNvbS91bmxvY2tpbmctc3RhYmxlLW9uLXBvbGljeS1ybC1vbi10cHUtd2l0aC1iaXR3aXNlLXplcm8tdHJhaW5pbmZlcmVuY2UtbWlzbWF0Y2gvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Source: developers.googleblog.com ↗</a></p>

[Google's Zero-TIM implementation in Tunix](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/ZwWTV8x7hQcaumeeW9hA0w/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTctYWl8Yml0d2lzZS16ZXJvLXJsLW9uLXRwdXMta2lsbGluZy10aGUtb2ZmLXBvbGljeS1nYXB8fGh0dHBzOi8vZGV2ZWxvcGVycy5nb29nbGVibG9nLmNvbS91bmxvY2tpbmctc3RhYmxlLW9uLXBvbGljeS1ybC1vbi10cHUtd2l0aC1iaXR3aXNlLXplcm8tdHJhaW5pbmZlcmVuY2UtbWlzbWF0Y2gvP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) achieves bitwise-identical logprobs between sampler and trainer at the same weights. The problem it solves: floating-point reduction order differences in bfloat16 change rounding, creating a hidden off-policy gap over multi-turn trajectories that destabilizes on-policy RL.

Verified at multi-host scale across 64 TPU v5p chips (DP8×TP8), this closes the numerical gap using consistent arithmetic operations and standardized reduction orders. For anyone running production RL stacks on TPUs, this is the difference between training that converges and training that silently drifts.
