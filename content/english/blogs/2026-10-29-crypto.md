<h2 id="moodys-clears-ripples-rlusd-for-institutional-reserves">Moody&#x27;s clears Ripple&#x27;s RLUSD for institutional reserves</h2>

Moody's evaluation of RLUSD rests on strict collateral mechanics rather than platform volume. The stablecoin currently sits at a $2.5 billion market cap, backed predominantly by cash and short-term US sovereign debt. These reserves are custodied with BNY (Aa1) under an explicit target coverage ratio of 103%.

The agency evaluated reserve segregation, bankruptcy remoteness, liquidity haircuts, and redemption turnaround windows. RLUSD already acts as liquidity ballast inside Ripple Prime and internal cross-border payment corridors. The rating provides institutional treasurers a quantitative mandate to hold RLUSD on balance sheets without taking unrated counterparty de-pegging risks. Moody's explicitly noted the rating is sensitive to coverage dips below parity, duration mismatches in the underlying Treasuries, or any degradation in BNY’s custodial credit profile.

Stripe launched Link Balance, an [autonomous-agent payment stack](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/3kVOSmytPZdXdPAtemR8QA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTAtMjktY3J5cHRvfHN0cmlwZS1idWlsZHMtdGhlLWNsZWFyaW5naG91c2UtZm9yLWF1dG9ub21vdXMtYWktYWdlbnRzfHxodHRwczovL3guY29tL1N0cmlwZUNyeXB0by9zdGF0dXMvMjExNTExNjY3MjQ2MTgwNzgwND91dG1fc291cmNlPWJyaWVmLXRlY2gtbmV3cy5jb20) allowing models to clear merchant transactions without prompting human supervisors for every transaction authorization. Key management is handed off to embedded, non-custodial Privy wallets, which sign transactions autonomously against user-defined budget boundaries.

```
[Fiat Inflow] 
      │
      ▼
[Stripe Onramp] ──► [Tempo Stablecoin] ──► [Privy Non-Custodial Key]
                                                     │
[Traditional Merchant] ◄── [Stripe Issuing Card] ◄──┘
```

The system issues standard single-use virtual debit cards via Stripe Issuing at point-of-sale. Because card execution interfaces with legacy Visa/Mastercard rails, merchants do not need custom on-chain integrations, web3 libraries, or stablecoin deposit addresses. Transaction adoption jumped 38x month-over-month, with AI shopping client Muse reporting more than 25% of its weekly active base routing checkouts through Link Balance.

Instead of displaying a static order curve to DEX aggregators, Fynd’s smart contracts observe incoming pool quotes in the routing memory pool. It dynamically sets the pool’s fee parameter to the exact decimal value that beats competing quotes while maximizing LP margin. In a backtest across 60 ETH/USDG trades on Robinhood, static 30 bps pools surrendered roughly 63% of real fee revenue by consistently undercutting unnecessarily or mispricing fills. Fynd claims its dynamic margin capture yields a 2.7x fee increase for LPs.
