Stablecoins have a last mile problem, and the Federal Reserve just wrote it down in black and white. A new FEDS Note walks through a US-to-Mexico transfer: the on-chain leg is instant, but converting to pesos and getting the money to the recipient still runs through a traditional bank that can charge opaque FX rates and fees. The blockchain replaced correspondent banks, not the banking system.

That's the real story of the day - the infrastructure everyone assumed would kill cross-border friction still depends on the exact institutions it was supposed to bypass. The Fed even draws a parallel to its own 1915 Gold Settlement Fund, which centralized settlement bookkeeping for the same reason. Engineers building on stablecoin rails should read the note closely: [the last mile is where the costs and the compliance live](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/DCwrMVXOUyZW3RQg7t-GwA/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMTktZmludGVjaHx8fGh0dHBzOi8vZmludGVjaHRha2VzLmNvbS9wL3RoZS1sYXN0LW1pbGUtcHJvYmxlbS1mb3Itc3RhYmxlY29pbnM_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t).

The two-ecosystem strategy is working: Square processed $76.4 billion for sellers (up 14%, its fastest growth since early 2023), and Cash App generated $2.07 billion of the profit.

But read the fine print. Cash App's growth is driven by Cash App Borrow, with an average loan limit of just $300. Consumer lending originations hit $19.0 billion, up 40% - and loan losses are up 83%. Block is now a lender with a payments business attached, using its bank charter to scale credit. That's the engine of the turnaround, and it's also the risk. The credit cycle has been benign for years; when it turns, those loss numbers get ugly fast.

The card payment flow after a swipe is a well-worn path: merchant request through Visa or Mastercard to the issuer, which approves or declines based on spending power and fraud risk. Issuers can now launch across 10-30 markets from a single dollar-denominated sponsor instead of a bank partner per country, and Visa and Mastercard accept stablecoin settlement seven days a week.

The catch: large domestic banks gain little from these "T-shaped" programs - wide across markets, deep in products. The disruption is aimed at the international layer, not the local giant.
