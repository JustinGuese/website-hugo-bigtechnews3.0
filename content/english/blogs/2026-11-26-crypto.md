Nvidia agreed to pay [$290 million](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/s8qYDAiGD1KQAQ0EQsED6Q/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjYtY3J5cHRvfG52aWRpYXMtMjkwbS1jcnlwdG8tbWluaW5nLXNldHRsZW1lbnQtaXMtYS1kaXNjbG9zdXJlLWNhdXRpb25hcnktdGFsZXx8aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhlYmxvY2suY28vbmV3cy9yZWd1bGF0aW9uLzIwMjYtMTEtMjUtbnZpZGlhLTI5MC1taWxsaW9uLXNldHRsZW1lbnQtY3J5cHRvLW1pbmluZy1jbGFzcy1hY3Rpb24tNDQ2NzA5P3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) to settle a class action alleging it understated how much crypto mining drove its 2017 and 2018 gaming GPU sales. The plaintiffs argued Nvidia's public statements misled investors about revenue exposure to a volatile market that later collapsed.

The number matters less than the mechanism: this is a disclosure case, not a fraud case. Nvidia didn't deny selling GPUs to miners; it denied that mining was a material part of the business. For any company with crypto-adjacent revenue, the lesson is that "we don't track that" isn't a defense. If your quarterly report mentions crypto demand, auditors will now assume you need to quantify it.

The senators' argument is that these exemptions create a "shadow stock market" that fragments liquidity and invites illicit finance. For engineers, the practical question is whether the exemption survives. If it does, expect more tokenized equity venues to launch under it; if it doesn't, every platform that built on the assumption of regulatory forbearance just took on serious legal risk. The SEC hasn't responded publicly.

Backpack Exchange launched an [RFQ system for stocks](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/c26_ohAn-_OKi07mWGAWmg/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjYtY3J5cHRvfGJhY2twYWNrLW9wZW5zLWl0cy1vcmRlci1mbG93LXRvLWFueS1tYXJrZXQtbWFrZXJ8fGh0dHBzOi8vdGhyZWFkcmVhZGVyYXBwLmNvbS90aHJlYWQvMjEyNTIxMjU3ODY3MDYzNzMxNi5odG1sP3V0bV9zb3VyY2U9YnJpZWYtdGVjaC1uZXdzLmNvbQ) that lets any market maker submit two-sided quotes competing directly with Backpack's own executing broker on each order. The system routes retail stock orders to whichever counterparty offers the best price at execution time.

This mirrors how RFQ works in traditional equity markets, where fragmented liquidity pools compete on price rather than posting to a single central order book. The consequence is straightforward: Backpack's internal broker no longer has a captive order flow. Every fill is now a competitive auction, which should tighten spreads but also means Backpack's own execution desk has to win on price, not on default routing.

A new [guide](https://ecommerce-api.datafortress.cloud/r/brieftechnews/xLH5y9Mu42q-A2zB1ixa-g/djF8d2VifDIwMjYtMTEtMjYtY3J5cHRvfGFhdmUtdnMtbW9ycGhvLXdoZXJlLXRvLWxpc3QteW91ci1hc3NldHx8aHR0cHM6Ly94LmNvbS9kZWZpbmlrb3Mvc3RhdHVzLzIxMjUyNDY3MDM4ODg0NzAyNTg_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1icmllZi10ZWNoLW5ld3MuY29t) compares Aave V4 and Morpho from an asset issuer's perspective, and the architectures couldn't be more different. Aave pools liquidity in Hubs that Spokes borrow from up to a cap, so unrecovered debt becomes a Hub deficit shared by all suppliers of that asset. Morpho's isolated markets with five fixed parameters keep bad debt inside one market and the curator vault that funded it.

The numbers: Morpho holds about $11.3B in deposits against $15.8B on Aave V3, with V4 recently passing $1B. During April's Kelp rsETH exploit, Aave lost $8.45B in deposits over 48 hours while Morpho's losses stayed contained in rsETH-exposed markets. The practical takeaway: if you're issuing a yield-bearing stablecoin, Morpho's isolation contains risk, but Aave's shared liquidity offers scale. For tokenized stocks, the recommendation is to follow your distribution partner - Coinbase's Aave V4 Spoke or Robinhood's Morpho markets.
